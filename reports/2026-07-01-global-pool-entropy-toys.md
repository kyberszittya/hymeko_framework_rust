## Mechanism

- Input is a non-graph point set: `(batch, points, 2)`.
- Global pooling is simultaneous over all point embeddings: `concat(mean, std, max)`.
- Entropy feedback model runs a first pooled classifier, computes normalized predictive entropy, broadcasts it back to every point, and applies a second simultaneous point update before the final pooled classifier.
- The training objective is final cross entropy plus `0.25 * first_pass_cross_entropy`.

## Results

These toy defaults are easy sanity checks: both the baseline global-pool set model and the entropy-feedback model reach perfect test accuracy on moons, rings, and XOR point-cloud problems.

## Readout

Entropy feedback works as a trainable global context loop and strongly lowers predictive entropy on these toy problems, but it costs roughly 2.3x to 3.0x more forward time than the single-pass global-pool baseline in this PyTorch harness. The current benchmark is therefore a mechanism check, not evidence of an accuracy win. Harder/noisier low-data variants are needed to tell whether entropy feedback improves sample efficiency.
