\section*{1.\quad The idea we are testing (the joint)}
The runtime monitor judges the task (reward-independent); CIP prioritizes reward\,$\leftrightarrow$\,monitor
disagreement; DirectLiNGAM proposes a causal graph over the rollout variables; \textbf{that graph is declared as a
\code{.hymeko} signed hypergraph and the HyMeKo engine re-derives its tensor view $+$ a Blake3 canonical hash} ---
so \emph{the causal model the agent uses is provably the one a human audits} (Kato--LiNGAM joint \#1). The science
extension on top is \textbf{LiNGAM-SH} (signed-hypergraph LiNGAM) --- the contribution proper, not integration glue.

\begin{center}\small\ttfamily
rollouts $\to$ HyMeKo monitor $\to$ CIP variables $\to$ DirectLiNGAM $\to$ causal DAG\\
DAG $\to$ declare as .hymeko $\to$ engine star/clique tensor $+$ Blake3 hash $\to$ cross-view: declared $\equiv$ tensor
\end{center}

\section*{3.\quad Results across the scenario}
\textbf{Coin-toss (de-risk).} \code{contact_score}\,\to\,\code{total_reward} ($+0.69$) is the strongest edge and
\code{total_reward} is disconnected from \code{delivery_score} $\Rightarrow$ \textbf{reward\_farming\_candidate}.
The \emph{intervention} (Stage~A, no training): recomputing the reward with the contact terms removed
\textbf{collapses that edge to $0.0$} and re-parents the reward onto \code{delivery_score} ($+0.88$). Supported at
the reward-computation level. We did \textbf{not} ``solve'' coin-toss (a physics wall and an imitation gap remain);
CIP \emph{diagnosed} it.

\medskip
\textbf{MetaWorld coffee-push (your scenario), multi-seed 5\,$\times$\,80, no training:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \code{near_fraction}\,$\leftrightarrow$\,\code{total_reward}: \textbf{CONFIRMED stable} --- present 5/5,
    $|w|$ median \textbf{0.973}, IQR $[0.962,0.981]$. The real MetaWorld reward is \textbf{proximity/contact-shaped}.
    (Honest: LiNGAM flips the direction 3/2 across batches --- coupling solid, direction not uniquely resolved.)
  \item \code{progress_score}\,\to\,\code{total_reward}: \textbf{rejected} as a direct edge (present 1/5) ---
    \code{near_fraction} mediates it. \emph{The single run suggested this edge; multi-seed removed it --- the value
    of aggregation.}
  \item Cross-view verification \textbf{passed in all 5/5 batches}; pass-rate median $0.51$, disagreement $0.35$.
\end{itemize}

\section*{5.\quad Honest scope (say this in the meeting)}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Every causal graph is \textbf{PROPOSED}; controlled ablation decides. The cross-view proves
    \emph{representation} consistency (declared $\equiv$ tensor $\equiv$ hash), \textbf{not} causal truth.
  \item MetaWorld's env randomization is \textbf{not controlled by the seed} --- single-run order is a point
    estimate; the coffee-push claim rests on the 5-batch aggregate, not one run.
  \item Real-env is \textbf{coffee-push only} so far (clean obs$\to$monitor mapping); dial-turn real-env needs a
    dial-angle extraction (its template is done).
  \item \textbf{No RL training} was run --- all read-only rollouts of cached / scripted policies.
\end{itemize}

\section*{6.\quad Proposed next steps (decisions for Kato)}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{LiNGAM-SH} --- constrain LiNGAM's $B$ to factor through the signed incidence
    ($B = A_{\mathrm{out}}\,\Sigma\,A_{\mathrm{in}}^{\top}$) so non-Gaussianity identifies the \emph{grouping} (the
    theorem). Nearest prior art (arXiv:2511.03831, CAM$\to$acyclic hypergraphs, Gaussian) validates the direction,
    does not pre-empt (no LiNGAM/ICA regime, no signs).
  \item \textbf{Real-env reward ablation on MetaWorld} --- transfer the coin Stage-A method: decompose the reward,
    recompute contact-off offline, check the $\to$\,\code{total_reward} edge collapses.
  \item \textbf{Multi-seed everywhere $+$ dial-turn real-env} for a publishable ranking claim.
\end{enumerate}
