## Mechanism — off-policy critic divergence (replicates the frozen v1 verdict)

Training trace: the critic **Q-value diverges monotonically negative** — `Q = −2.2 → −4.3 → −6.3 → −8.3` through
the critic warm-up, then `−9.7 → −12.8 → −17.4 → −23.1`. The actor is frozen during warm-up (ft_dom held 0.75 at

imitation baseline. The graded-contact **safety held** — the collapse is **not** reward-hacking (exploit 0,
