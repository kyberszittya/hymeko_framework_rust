# 6D-1 — obstacle reach, EE-route-selection benchmark

The falsifiable test the O2/Track-B result left open: **does the multimodal proposal help when the working solutions are
genuinely distant, geometrically-separated routes** (not the near-symmetric box case where search alone sufficed)? A
contact-free obstacle isolates this from contact physics / friction / unstable teacher / retention / object rotation /
mesh+inertia error.

## Claim scope (narrow, per the honest framing)
This is an **EE-route-selection** benchmark: the obstacle is `contype=0` (never blocks the arm physically) and collision
is graded by the **end-effector path's AABB intersection**. Route modes are **geometrically-separated / locally-
disconnected proposal basins** (a left-mode's local jitter cannot slip to the right/over route — verified), NOT strict
homotopy classes (in free 3-D they are continuously deformable). It tests *can the structured multimodal runtime select
among EE route families in an obstructed pose-reach?* It does **NOT** claim full-arm collision avoidance (that needs link–
obstacle collision, self-collision, joint limits, the executed EE path, and real SE(3) orientation constraints — later).

## Validity gates (all PASS before any grid — the panel is fixed, controller-independent)
- **Direct path blocked:** 40/40 (obstacle seated at the start↔goal midpoint).
- **State-dependent route families:** the OPEN routes vary by state — seed 6 feasible {left,right}, seed 44 feasible
  {over,under} (opposite). A single fixed detour cannot solve all states; the proposal must select from the state.
- **Execution-based eligibility** (NOT the geometric polyline — which is unreliable: an AABB-clean via can be
  kinematically unreachable or the servo cuts the corner). Eligible = direct blocked ∧ ≥1 execution-feasible route ∧ ≥1
  execution-infeasible route. **14 eligible / 60 scanned (rate 0.23)**; reported, not policy-selected (no cherry-picking).
- **Mode non-crossing:** a mode's jittered candidates stay in its route-family basin — min 1.000.
- **Realised allocation** (requested prob-reweighting → integer per-mode counts): `top_probe` B=12,K=4 = **[9,1,1,1]**,
  `equal` = [3,3,3,3], `prob` = [5,3,2,2]. Exactly as intended.
- **Critical pair** (same state/budget/RNG/executor/cert): single-head anchored to an execution-INFEASIBLE route
  **2/14**; K-mode covering all routes **13/14** → `SINGLE_WRONG_MODE_FAILS_KMODE_RECOVERS`. (The 2/14 are feasibility-
  labeling noise near the boundary — the 3-trial eligibility classifier vs the 12-budget critical executor; the 2-vs-13
  separation is overwhelming.)

## Fair deploy grid — K-mode strictly beats single-head at EQUAL budget
Single-head@argmax (K=1) vs K-mode × allocation, at equal total budget B, over the 14-state eligible panel, 2 search-
seeds (PILOT). B=0 = the direct proposal (no search). Best-K-mode per budget:

| B | single-head K1 | K-mode (best) | Δ (equal budget) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0.643 | 0.643 (no search — K inert) | 0 |
| 4 | 0.643 | 0.929 (K3) | **+0.286** |
| 8 | 0.679 | 1.000 (K3 top_probe) | **+0.321** |
| 12 | 0.714 | 1.000 (K3 equal) | **+0.286** |
| 24 | 0.714 | 1.000 (K3/K4) | **+0.286** |

**Single-head plateaus at ~0.71** — extra budget cannot help because local jitter cannot cross basins, so it is capped at
*how often the argmax route is right*. **K-mode rises to 1.0** — coverage + search finds the feasible route. The advantage
is large (+0.29), consistent across every budget, and appears at low budget (B=4), not only high budget. Allocation is
second-order (equal / top_probe / prob all work once K covers the feasible route); K3 already saturates (≤4 routes, 1–3
feasible). Figure `obstacle_6d1_grid.png`; GIF `obstacle_6d1.gif` (the arm routing around the obstacle).

**Verdict:** `MULTIMODAL_POLICY_SEARCH_VALIDATED_ON_DISJOINT_PATH_MODES` (PILOT).

## The two results together — the precise scoping of when multimodality is a deploy lever
| | Track B (O2 boxes) | 6D-1 (obstacle) |
|---|---|---|
| basin structure | near-symmetric — winning θ CLOSE, one connected basin | genuinely separated route families, locally DISCONNECTED |
| single-head + budget | finds the good option (search load-bearing) | **capped at argmax-correctness (~0.71) — cannot cross** |
| K-mode at equal budget | **no advantage** (splitting starves the search) | **+0.29, saturates to 1.0** |

The multimodal proposal is a deploy lever **when and only when the solution modes are geometrically-separated basins that
local search cannot cross.** On connected-basin problems (boxes) search alone suffices; on disconnected-basin problems
(the obstacle) modality is essential. This VALIDATES the O2-motivated architecture and scopes it precisely — and confirms
the reading that O2's wall was contact candidate-localization within a connected basin, not a failure of the runtime.
