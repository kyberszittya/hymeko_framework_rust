## The idea

Phase B fixed the one-sided crab with a hard mirror-equivariance, but the mirror was **hand-coded** —
and for the structured (per-vertex hypergraph) obs I could not derive it reliably (an exhaustive search
over feature-flips found none that worked; the HSiKAN separately had a null residual). The user's idea
closes this cleanly: **the left-right reflection is an automorphism of the HyMeKo-generated signed
kinematic hypergraph** — so read the symmetry *from the structure* the control language emits, rather
than guessing it per obs-representation.

## Result — the reflection is an EXACT signed automorphism, derived from labels alone

The HyMeKo AIBO structure (33 vertices) labels its parts `*_fl/*_fr`, `*_bl/*_br`, `*_l/*_r` with midline
parts unsuffixed. The permutation `σ` that swaps those side-tokens (midline → itself), derived from the
**labels only**, is:

- an **involution** (`σ² = id`);
- an **exact signed-incidence automorphism**: **0 / 44** edges violate `edge(σu,σv).sign == edge(u,v).sign`
  — every signed kinematic arc maps to a signed arc with the same sign;
- midline-fixed: `torso, mid_body, rear_body, tail{_seg,,_tip}, neck, head{,_gz,_gy}, muzzle, nose, jaw`;
  swapped pairs: the four legs' `hip_bracket/thigh/shin/paw`, plus `eye_l/r`, `ear_l/r`.

Its induced action on the abduction (actuator) vertices `[fl,fr,bl,br]` is **`[1,0,3,2]`** — *exactly* the
`fl↔fr, bl↔br` permutation the hand-coded `mirror_act = −a[[1,0,3,2]]` used. **The mirror I was guessing
is the HyMeKo structure's automorphism.** So the equivariance prior — the obs vertex-permutation *and* the
action-space group action — is **generated by the control language and verified**, not hand-authored.

## Why this matters

- **It removes the hand-coding / guessing.** The Phase-B mirror worked for the flat MLP but was
  unreliable for the structured obs; the structural automorphism is exact and representation-correct by
  construction, and it is **verified** (a violated automorphism raises — design by contract).
- **It is the HyMeKo thesis, concretely.** The control-language structure *is* the source of the
  invariance: the same signed hypergraph that drives HSiKAN message-passing also **defines the symmetry
  group** the policy must respect. Structure as topology and structure as equivariance are one object.
- **It generalises.** `structural_reflection(hg)` reads any HyMeKo signed hypergraph; a humanoid or a
  different quadruped exposes its own reflection (and, in principle, larger automorphism groups — `S_n`
  over interchangeable limbs) with no per-robot mirror code.

## Scope / what this is not (yet)

This delivers + verifies the **symmetry primitive**; it does not by itself produce a two-sided crab. The
end-to-end use is the culmination: **train a hard-equivariant HSiKAN whose equivariance is this structural
automorphism** (symmetrization inside the actor, mirror = `σ` on the hg obs + the induced action perm +
lateral-feature parity). That pairs the two open threads — Phase B (equivariance fixes the crab, for a
policy with an active crab) and the HSiKAN null-residual diagnostic (needs to learn an active crab) — with
the structural mirror supplying the exact group action. Recorded as the next step.
