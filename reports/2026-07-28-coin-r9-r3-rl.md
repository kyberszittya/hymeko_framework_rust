## Diagnosis (three contributing causes, none fatal)

1. **Actor-warm-start with fresh critics (an honest fallback, not a full continuation).** The R2 driver saved only metrics, not
   the full training state — so, per the contract, R3 could only regenerate the R2 champion actor and resume with **fresh
   critics / replay / exploration**. Fresh critics discard R2's learned value function; the new exploration found a
   clamp-stall local optimum. A **true full continuation** (R2's critics + replay, fresh RNG) is the right fix and is expected
   to behave far better.
2. **The champion key is stall-blind.** The lexicographic champion (safety ≻ K6 ≻ released ≻ −exit_dtz ≻ −min_dtz ≻ −jerk) has
   no term for stall / negative-v_par / heavy-clamp, so it **selected the stalling actor** (exit 46.89 mm) over a cleaner one.
   The champion must rank a clean moving exit above a stalled one (add a "no stall / light Fn / +v_par" criterion above
   contact-exit dtz).
3. **Frontier-region RL credit.** Consistent with the R3-B hypothesis: the policy loses the coin around 48–55 mm, and uniform
   full-chain episodes give that region little credit. The frontier curriculum (branch snapshots at 48–58 mm, +v_par, light
   contact — legal, no state edit; 50 % entry / 50 % frontier) is the sanctioned next step.

Update-zero identity, matched conditions, determinism (bit-identical replay), and safety all held; the failure is a bad local
optimum, not a harness bug.

## Recommendation for R3-B (awaiting your green)

Per your plateau path, R3-B is warranted — but the R3-A finding says to fix two things first so R3-B is not sabotaged by the
same issues:

1. **Full continuation, not fresh critics** — extend `train_perstep` to checkpoint/resume the full state (actor, twin critics +

2. **Stall-aware champion** — add `(no_stall ∧ light_exit_fn ∧ v_par>0)` above the contact-exit-dtz criterion so a clean moving
   exit always beats a stalled one.
3. **Then the frontier curriculum** — 50 % KINETIC-entry / 50 % legal frontier snapshots (48–58 mm, +v_par, light), branched
   from real rollouts (no state edit), to put RL credit where the coin is currently lost.

Hard-stop still respected: total extra options so far is +300 (not the +600 cap); contact-exit did not reach < 40 mm, so —
