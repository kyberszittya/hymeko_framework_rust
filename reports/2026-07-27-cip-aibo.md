## User request: "it would be ridiculous if it could not turn" — addressed

The base `QuadrupedTrotGait` walks straight only. This scenario adds a scripted
`SteeredTrotGait` (differential left/right stride ⇒ yaw; `drive=0` ⇒ PD
stand-hold). **Measured yaw authority: −3.64 °/s** at `yaw_cmd=−0.5` (and up to
~168°/1000 steps at full command), torso upright — the Aibo **genuinely turns**.
Honest limit: the scripted yaw is stable in **one** direction; full command the
other way destabilizes it, so robust arbitrary-bearing pursuit needs gait tuning
(or the branch's SAC walk policy). Recorded, not hidden.
