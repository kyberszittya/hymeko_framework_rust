## Acceptance

- ✅ **DAgger improves ft_dom over each architecture's own (same-run) BC0**: MLP **0.208 → 0.514** (+0.306),
  HSiKAN **0.215 → 0.354** (+0.139) on best-checkpoint. DAgger is useful for both; the lever past the BC0 ceiling
  is imitation (as on v1).
- ❌ **HSiKAN advantage is NOT confirmed under DAgger.** MLP+DAgger beats HSiKAN+DAgger on both best-checkpoint
  (0.514 vs 0.354) and validation-selected (0.452 vs 0.250) fingertip_dominant. **MLP is the reliable DAgger
  architecture.** This **replicates the v1 finding** (v1: MLP+DAgger 0.653 reliable > HSiKAN+DAgger 0.570 noisy,
  val picking BC0 for HSiKAN).
- ✅ **No exploit-driven raw** (exploit ≤ 0.021 at deploy).
- **Validation selection (24-ep) works for MLP, not HSiKAN.** MLP: 2/3 optimal, val-selected 0.452 captures ~88%
  of the best-ckpt 0.514. HSiKAN: 1/3 optimal — on two seeds val picks BC0, missing the DAgger peaks, so
  val-selected 0.250 barely exceeds its BC0 0.215. The larger 24-ep selector helped MLP but HSiKAN's curve is
  **too noisy for even 24-ep validation to select** — best-ckpt (oracle) is 0.354 but the deployable
  val-selected is only 0.250.

## Caveat (honest, must not be conflated)

This DAgger run **re-trained** BC0 for each architecture (same seed); under the RL/stochastic carve-out (CUDA
non-determinism) the re-trained **HSiKAN BC0 came out 0.215** here versus the **frozen v2 BC0 of 0.361**
(`reports/2026-07-07-v2-bc0.md`). So HSiKAN started this DAgger run from an unlucky, weaker BC0. The DAgger
**lift** (best-ckpt − same-run-BC0) is the internally-consistent measure and still favours MLP (+0.306 vs +0.139);
the absolute HSiKAN numbers here sit on a weaker BC0 than the frozen baseline. A cleaner HSiKAN read would fix
the BC0 seed/weights before the DAgger rounds (warm-start from the frozen BC0) rather than re-training it.

## Branch interpretation (do NOT read as "HSiKAN is bad" / "drop HSiKAN")

The correct reading: **under the current v2 DAgger setup, HSiKAN did not confirm an advantage over MLP; MLP+DAgger
is the reliable deployable branch.** HSiKAN has previously shown high peaks (near-scripted in earlier runs), so it
stays as a **structured-representation hypothesis, not the current main baseline**. Two branches:

1. **Main reliable branch — MLP+DAgger.** Selected deployable checkpoint (ft_dom 0.452); the first RL
   initialization baseline.
2. **Structured HSiKAN research branch.** Not currently deployable (high-variance, not validation-selectable in
   the present setup), but **kept** — working hypothesis: HSiKAN may benefit specifically from **direct coupling
   to the HyMeKo-generated structured tensor representation**, rather than a mostly flattened/manual observation.

**Queued HSiKAN-specific test (NOT "rerun and hope" — test the structure hypothesis):**
- **A.** current baseline — flattened-observation HSiKAN.
- **B.** HyMeKo tensor-connected HSiKAN — observation from the HyMeKo tensor schema with role/entity/group
  structure preserved, contact-quality tensor included, graph/hypergraph relation tensor included if available.
- **C.** (optional) HSiKAN actor on the structured obs tensor + centralized CTDE critic on the HyMeKo privileged
  state tensor.
- **Acceptance:** the tensor-connected version improves **≥1 of** {mean fingertip_dominant, validation-selected
  performance, cross-seed variance, checkpoint selectability} **without increasing** body_assisted /
  body_driven_exploit / body_only_progress. The question is whether HSiKAN benefits *specifically* from the
  HyMeKo-generated structured representation — not whether a rerun gets lucky.
