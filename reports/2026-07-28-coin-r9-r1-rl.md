## Event trace + reward decomposition (the honest mechanism)

Best-TD3 residual vs the clone on the KINETIC transport:

| step | clone dtz / v_par | TD3-res dtz / v_par |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 77.99 / 0.194 | 77.99 / 0.194 |
| 4 | 68.90 / 0.355 | 68.51 / 0.381 |
| 6 (last gripped) | 61.42 / 0.366 | 60.62 / **0.390** |
| coast landing | **46.2 mm** | **39.6 mm** |

The forward-heavy residual (`+0.996` on the lead push dim) builds **slightly more momentum** (v_par peak 0.370 → 0.390) over
the *same* 6 gripped steps, so the free coast carries the coin **farther — 46.2 → 39.6 mm — closer to the zone, not past it**
(min_dtz 39.6 ≫ the 20 mm zone edge; no overshoot). This is what the reward rewards: the progress term (+6.65) is landing-based,
**not** raw v_par (the reward has no v_par magnitude term — v_par is only kept positive by the stall penalty), so the RL could
not win by flinging the coin. Both algos still incur `early_loss` (−12): they exit bilateral contact at ~60 mm (> 55 mm), so
they improve by coasting farther, **not** yet by holding contact to the corridor. Holding contact below ~55 mm (the K1-A bank's
coverage floor) is the state-dependent skill the **per-step** residual (R2) + more budget must learn — the constant residual
cannot.
