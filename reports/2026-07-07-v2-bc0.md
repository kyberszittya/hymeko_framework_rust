the cloning gap (DAgger) is what raises the clones' clean delivery — and reward — toward the scripted ceiling.

## Interpretation (rules applied)

- **fingertip_dominant is the headline metric.** HSiKAN **0.361** > MLP **0.194** — HSiKAN clones the two-finger
  delivery structure substantially better and with **lower variance** (raw std 0.034 vs 0.104).
- **Neither architecture learns the exploit** — body_driven_exploit is **0.000** for all 6 clones, and
  body_only_progress is ~0.002 (negligible). BC of the fingertip demonstrator under the v2b reward produces
  fingertip-attributed delivery, exactly as intended. Raw delivery here is *not* body-driven (exploit 0), so it
  equals fingertip_dominant + a small assisted fraction.
- **v2 BC0 is on a HARDER task than v1** and must not be conflated: the physical-contact scene dropped even the
  scripted ceiling from ~0.85 (v1) to 0.75 (v2). Relative to ceiling, HSiKAN BC0 clones ≈48% (0.361/0.75) vs the
  v1 ≈60% (0.514/0.85) — the coin bumping off the arms during approach is genuinely harder to clone. The v1 BC0
  numbers are labeled *v1 passthrough* and are a different scene.
- **RL stays frozen; no DAgger yet** (this is the BC0 baseline report only). The natural next lever past the BC0
  ceiling is DAgger (imitation), on this same v2 graded scene + v2b reward, best-checkpoint selection, a larger
  validation selector than the prior 12-episode one.
