# TD3 — twin critics, delayed policy, target smoothing (P3), as a config of the off-policy core

**Date:** 2026-06-21 · **Author:** Aiko (Claude Code) for Dr. Csaba Hajdu
**Plan:** [docs/plans/2026-06-21-rl-algorithm-architecture/](../docs/plans/2026-06-21-rl-algorithm-architecture/) (P3) ·
**Prior:** [reports/2026-06-21-ddpg-offpolicy.md](2026-06-21-ddpg-offpolicy.md)

## Summary
TD3 is implemented — **not as a forked trainer but as three config axes of the DDPG core** (§6.5 #1). One
`train_offpolicy` + `OffPolicyConfig`; TD3 = DDPG plus, each a named fix to DDPG's Q-overestimation/fragility:
- `n_critics=2` → **clipped double-Q** (the Bellman target uses the `min` over the twin target critics);
- `policy_delay=2` → **delayed** actor + target updates;
- `target_noise>0` → **target-policy smoothing** (clipped Gaussian on the target action).

DDPG is the degenerate preset (`n_critics=1, policy_delay=1, target_noise=0`); `run_ddpg`/`run_td3` are thin
presets; CLI `--algo {ddpg,td3}`.

## The honest (non-)result on cart-pole
| algo (mlp, seed 0, 20k steps) | untrained | final upright | curve (4k…20k) |
|---|---|---|---|
| DDPG | 27.3 | **199.4** | `[121, 198, 200, 200, 199]` |
| **TD3** | 32.3 | **114.1** | `[142, 66, 89, 200, 113]` |

TD3 **learns** (reaches 200 at 16k) but its single-seed run is **noisier** and **ended lower** than DDPG —
the curve oscillates and degrades late. So on the forgiving cart-pole, TD3's extra machinery buys **nothing
visible over DDPG**, and on this one seed looks worse.

**This is the expected non-result, not a failure.** TD3's documented benefit is *stability and robustness
across seeds and on harder tasks* (it fixes DDPG's divergence on tasks where DDPG actually diverges). Cart-pole
is easy enough that DDPG never diverges, so there is no failure for TD3 to fix; and a single seed cannot show a
variance/robustness improvement. The same structural limitation as the architecture question: **cart-pole is
the wrong task to judge TD3 vs DDPG.** The proper comparison is multi-seed and/or on a harder task (the 6-DOF
arm, or a MuJoCo locomotion env) where DDPG's instability appears.

## What was built / changed
- `hymeko_rl/ddpg.py` — unified: `OffPolicyConfig` (DDPG fields + the three TD3 axes, defaulting to DDPG),
  `build_offpolicy(…, n_critics)` → `(actor, [critic…])`, `train_offpolicy` (clipped double-Q + delay +
  smoothing), `td3_config()` preset, `run_offpolicy`/`run_ddpg`/`run_td3`, `--algo` CLI. `DDPGConfig` kept as a
  back-compat alias.
- The truncation-as-non-terminal correctness point and the swappable backbone (mlp/hsikan/signedkan) carry over
  unchanged.

## Test results
- `test_ddpg.py` — **7 tests**: replay ring; actor bounded + critic scalar (mlp **and** hsikan); TD3 builds
  twin critics + the `td3_config` preset values; `train_offpolicy` runs end-to-end **parametrized over both
  DDPG and TD3** configs (so the clipped-double-Q / delay / smoothing paths are exercised).
- **Regression:** the full `test_ppo`/`test_policy`/`test_inverted_pendulum_env` suite — **33 passed, 1
  skipped** — so the `eval_balance → GreedyPolicy` Protocol retype and the DDPG→off-policy refactor broke
  nothing. `ruff` clean; `mypy --strict` clean (the endemic `mujoco` import-untyped only).

## §6.5 anti-patterns
None — and one *avoided*: DDPG and TD3 are **one config-driven trainer**, not the two separate files the plan
sketched. The TD3 axes are config fields, not a forked function family (§6.5 #1).
