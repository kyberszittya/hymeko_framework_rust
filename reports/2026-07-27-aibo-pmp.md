## What transfers

legged body is a Linear Inverted Pendulum. The same reduced LIPM/PMP core — Hamiltonian matrix

Hamiltonian-matrix `P` matches `solve_continuous_are` to < 1e-6. The optimal COM force (x **and**
y) is applied through the quadruped's COM Jacobian on top of a leg PD-stand, **under the motion
contract**.

- **The transfer is real and PHYSICAL.** Unlike the retracted capture-widening (26.9 rad/s,
