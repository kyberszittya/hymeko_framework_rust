\section{Introduction / motivation}
The task is \textbf{collaborative contact-rich coin-pushing}: two planar 2-link arms must push a cylinder
(``coin'') into a target zone. It is a minimal testbed for \emph{cooperative} contact manipulation where a single
agent is physically insufficient.

\textbf{Why closed-loop imitation failure matters.} A behaviour-cloned (BC) policy can have tiny per-step action
error yet fail catastrophically at deployment through closed-loop compounding (covariate shift). Diagnosing this
is a prerequisite to any reinforcement learning (RL): if the imitation prior is unstable, RL refinement inherits
and amplifies the instability.

\textbf{Why reward/physics validation is required before RL.} An RL run is only meaningful if (i) the physics
actually requires the intended behaviour (here: two coordinated fingertips), and (ii) the reward's optimum is the
intended task (delivery), not a farmable proxy. We gate every RL launch on these before spending compute.

\section{Task and simulation}
\textbf{Environment.} \fn{PlanarGraspEnv} (\fn{hymeko\_rl/env/planar\_grasp\_env.py}): two hand-authored 2-link
$+Z$-hinge arms on a table; a coin (cylinder, radius $0.035$) on slide-x/slide-y/hinge-z joints; a target zone
(radius $0.055$). Observation is per-vertex features on the two-arm hypergraph $(N,8)$; action is $4$ joint-position
targets. Episodes end on sustained in-zone dwell (success), coin knocked out of bounds (death), or
\fn{max\_steps}$=300$.

\textbf{Two-fingertip requirement (collision design).} \meas{} The coin collides \emph{only} with the fingertip
geoms (bitmask: coin \fn{contype/conaffinity}$=2/2$, arm links MuJoCo default $1/1$, fingertips $1/3$); the arm
\emph{bodies pass through} the coin by design. \fn{coin\_frictionloss} defaults to $0$ (free slide, only
velocity-damping $2.5$). Thus the two-arm requirement is enforced by \emph{contact geometry} (a single point
contact on a round free coin is laterally unstable), \interp{} not by a friction force threshold (see \S3).

\textbf{Scripted controller.} \fn{PushDemonstrator} (\fn{hymeko\_rl/experiments/galambos\_demo.py}): $k$ fingertips
form a slot fan behind the coin along the coin$\to$zone ray and plow it in; press scales down inside
\fn{brake\_dist} so the coin settles. FSM declared in \fn{data/robotics/galambos\_push.hymeko} (phases
\fn{approach}, \fn{push}, \fn{hold}).

\textbf{Phases (analysis buckets).} APPROACH ($=$\fn{approach}, orbit to slots) / CONTACT (both fingertips
touching) / PUSH ($=$\fn{push}, dist $\ge$ \fn{brake\_dist}$=0.06$) / BRAKE ($=$\fn{push} near-zone $+$ \fn{hold}).

\textbf{Evaluation protocol.} Delivery $=$ fraction of episodes where the coin is \emph{held} in the zone for
\fn{success\_steps} (dwell, not a momentary touch). Auxiliary per-step metrics: \fn{dist\_delta} $=d_0-d_{\text{final}}$
(coin$\to$zone, $>0$ closer), \fn{coin\_vel\_to\_zone} $=\mathbb{}$ mean $\dot{x}_{\text{coin}}\cdot\hat{u}_{\text{zone}}$
($>0$ toward target), \fn{fingertip\_contact}, \fn{both\_contact}. Reward for all coin RL/BC below:
\fn{data/robotics/galambos\_task\_deliver.hymeko} (deliver $+$ \fn{zone\_progress}, oracle certify $=$ True).

\section{Current interpretation}
\interp{}
\begin{enumerate}[nosep]
\item DAgger is the \textbf{confirmed} correction for BC covariate shift (MLP: BC0 $0.278\to$ best-ckpt $0.653$,
all 3 seeds).
\item \textbf{MLP$+$DAgger is reliable on the mean} (best-ckpt $0.653\pm0.085$; lower variance).
\item \textbf{HSiKAN$+$DAgger has a higher observed peak} ($0.792$, near scripted $0.854$) \textbf{but high
variance} (best-ckpt $0.570\pm0.161$); the architecture advantage under naive BC is partly a covariate-shift
artifact, and its post-DAgger superiority is \emph{not} established.
\item \textbf{Frozen-trunk DAgger is harmful} (D1 crater; negative mechanism result).
\item \textbf{No-freeze DAgger removes the frozen-trunk pathology but is negative on the mean}: best-ckpt
$0.639\pm0.138$ (ties MLP $0.653$, above plain $0.570$ and frozen $0.528$), yet held-out val-selection picks BC0 in
all 3 seeds ($\Rightarrow$ selected $=0.514$, no net gain), and the $0.792$ peak is not val-selectable.
\item \textbf{RL remains frozen} until imitation is stable (no learned collaborative RL has beaten the scripted
$0.854$; best learned so far $0.792$, a single HSiKAN$+$DAgger seed).
\end{enumerate}

\section{Evidence-completeness rule and checkpoint-persistence gap}
\textbf{Rule (2026-07-06).} A learned-policy experiment is \emph{evidence-complete} only if it persists: policy
checkpoint, architecture/config, seed, reward file, eval protocol, artifact path, aggregate metrics, and
representative rollout evidence (trajectory $+$ distance-over-time $+$ GIF/frames).

\textbf{Gap (recorded --- NOT a failure of the scientific result).} \meas{} The DAgger diagnostic harness
(\fn{scratchpad/dagger.py}) recorded delivery numbers but did not call \fn{torch.save}. The DAgger-stage
checkpoints (MLP$+$DAgger best, plain HSiKAN$+$DAgger best, no-freeze selected, frozen-trunk) were never
