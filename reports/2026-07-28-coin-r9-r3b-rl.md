## The hybrid-boundary correction (central result)

R3-A's frontier restart from the 61.42 mm clone snapshot failed **not** as generic plumbing but as
**`FRONTIER_RESTART_FAILURE_DUE_TO_HYBRID_GUARD_BOUNDARY_ALIASING`**: the same continuous state behaves differently arriving
*inside* a running KINETIC trajectory versus *restarted* as a fresh episode, because the control situation is the full hybrid
state `(x, mode, controller/clone history, guard latch)`, not `x` alone. Two defects compounded:

1. **Off-by-one capture** — the env was captured *post*-step (s_{t+1}) while the label came from the *pre*-step log (dtz_t), so
   a "61 mm frontier" actually held the contact-loss state. **Fixed**: capture the PRE-step causal state (s_t + clone hidden
   h_{t-1} + previous residual + prev_tau), verified by a **one-step restart identity of 7 µm** (bit-tight; multi-step
   divergence is chaotic contact dynamics, not a capture error).
2. **Missing restart-admissibility margin** — a frontier must be a KINETIC *interior*, not merely a frame with contact. **Fixed**:
   the healthy predicate requires KINETIC mode, +v_par above the envelope floor, light non-degenerate contact (Fn ≥ 0.12 N),
   no stall, no sign reversal, ≥ 2 frames since mode entry, a **fully restorable causal state**, and a **restart-invariance
   probe under the reaching policy** yielding ≥ 2 KINETIC steps. (Probing with a *zero* residual is wrong — it rejects exactly
   the residual-held states the curriculum wants, since the clone alone cannot hold contact past its own ~61 mm limit.)

**Frontier audit (from the clean R2 champion):** 2 healthy — 57.3 mm (v_par 0.357, Fn 0.222, 6 frames-since-entry, restart 3),
53.84 mm (v_par 0.324, Fn 0.123, 7 frames, restart 2); **0 boundary-misclassified**. The 61 mm edge state is correctly
**rejected** as a boundary/terminal-risk state (restart 1 < 2) — verified in the tests.
