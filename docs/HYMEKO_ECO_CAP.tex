\section*{The standard this document holds itself to}
\addtocounter{section}{0}
Each row separates three things on purpose: \textbf{the specific claim}, \textbf{the
artifact that checks it}, and --- where it matters --- \textbf{what the artifact does
not establish}. Any line can be audited by opening the named file.

\noindent\fbox{\parbox{\dimexpr\textwidth-2\fboxsep-2\fboxrule\relax}{%
\textbf{Two honesty rules.}\quad
(1)~A claim is only as strong as what is \emph{mechanically} checked: ``a test passes''
means its own assertions hold, and does not silently extend to properties the test never
examines. Where a test proves structure but not visual fidelity or numerical parity, the
row or the section note says so.\quad
(2)~\textbf{Provenance is marked.} Results re-verified this session are tagged
\emph{(measured)}; results resting on a prior dated report are attributed to it rather
than presented as freshly confirmed.
\par\smallskip
\textbf{Status legend.}\quad
\sProven{} --- a passing test or committed artifact asserts the specific claim.\quad
\sShipped{} --- works in a demo/report but is not pinned by a regression test, so it can
regress silently.\quad
\sResearch{} --- an open investigation, reported as positive, partial, null, or falsified
--- whichever it is.
\par\smallskip
A status is never upgraded without an anchor; a row with a stale anchor is a defect, not a
convenience.}}

\section{Research lines --- reported as found}
\begin{captable}
Signed-link prediction; leakage audit (label-shuffle, G\"omb-strict)
  & Audit harness + vectorisation ($\sim$6$\times$) + figure done; 5-seed grid running. The
    contribution is the \emph{audit protocol}; results in progress.
  & \sResearch\\
\addlinespace
HSiKAN geometric / triad attention; rotor tuning
  & A/B head built; closing a $\sim$0.02--0.03 AUROC gap to SiGAT is the open question,
    attributed (untested) to attention.
  & \sResearch\\
\addlinespace
HyMeYOLO / Soma-vision detection
  & \textbf{Mostly negative:} Soma-vision falsified for vision in the backlog; held-out VOC mAP
    $\sim$0.015. Reported as a negative result, not a capability.
  & \sResearch{} \qual{(negative)}\\
\addlinespace
\textbf{Cayley/Clifford rotor embedding} (inductive, leakage-free)
  & \textbf{Positive, honest framing:} Pareto-efficient on signed-link --- near-best AUROC at
    $\sim$270$\times$ fewer parameters, leakage-free under label-shuffle, 5 datasets $\times$ 5
    seeds. Beats DADSGNN/SGCN; trails SiGAT by $\sim$0.02 at that size --- that trade \emph{is}
    the result. 9 unit tests.
  & \sResearch{} \qual{(validated)}\\
\addlinespace
S$^1$ \code{(cos,sin)} rotor encoding of RL \textbf{joint angles}
  & \textbf{Falsified, narrowly:} one ablation arm. Revolute joints are range-limited
    ($\pm2.5$ rad), never reach the $\pm\pi$ wrap, so the periodicity buys nothing (smoke:
    0.329 vs 0.240 m). Does \emph{not} bear on the primitive above --- different mechanism,
    different domain. Reverted.
  & \sFalsified{} \qual{(scoped)}\\
\end{captable}
\scopenote{Reading §9 fairly. The two rotor rows are deliberately distinct: the embedding
\emph{primitive} is a validated parameter-efficiency result; the RL joint-angle \emph{encoding}
is a single falsified ablation. Neither implies the other. The vision line is reported as
negative on purpose --- a ledger that hid its null results would not be worth reading.}
