\begin{abstract}
\noindent R11.0 (\done, \code{021a1e54}) proved a positive control: from the exact zero reset a Cartesian reach + a
READY-specific CEM capture + the frozen downstream delivers strict K6 (min\_dtz 11.54\,mm, coin untouched 0.0\,mm, first
frame $[0,0,0,0]$). R11.1 (\done) replaced the fragile 3-shell reach with a robust 4-DOF RRT-Connect that generalizes over
the coin grid (9/10, $\sim$0.5\,s). The remaining bottleneck is \emph{not} the reach but \textbf{capture/delivery
generalization}: the learned R2 residual and the handoff orbit are canonical-only, so a coin shifted $\geq$2--3\,cm follows
the wrong orbit and misses (28--66\,mm). R11 (this plan) builds the rest: a HyMeKo hybrid IR (initial-condition
certificate, modes $M_0\!\to\!M_7$, transitions with handoff + energy descriptors), a coin/target demonstration bank, a
\textbf{coin/target-conditioned teacher-free} capture--delivery policy (BC $\to$ reward-driven RL), and joint refinement of
reach geometry, timing, hybrid-transition smoothness, and \textbf{Hamiltonian energy flow}. Every result starts from
$q=[0,0,0,0]$ in one continuous episode; strict K6, safety, no-premature-contact, and no-teleport are hard constraints.
\textbf{This is a program-level plan; implementation is gated, one boundary at a time, R11.2 first.}
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope and goal}
\textbf{Goal.} A robust, coin/target-conditioned, \emph{teacher-free} hybrid policy that generalizes from the exact zero
home over $x_{\mathrm{coin}}\!\sim\!\mathcal D_{\mathrm{coin}}$, $x_{\mathrm{target}}\!\sim\!\mathcal D_{\mathrm{target}}$
and, subject to strict K6 + safety, minimizes path length, total $T_{\mathrm{ZERO}\to\mathrm{K6}}$, transition jerk/jumps,
and Hamiltonian energy cost. \textbf{Deployed episode (one continuous rollout):}
\begin{center}\itshape EXACT\_ZERO\_HOME $\to$ collision-free reach $\to$ precontact alignment $\to$ capture $\to$
controlled delivery $\to$ target entry $\to$ settle $\to$ strict K6.\end{center}
\textbf{Non-goals / hard stops.} No READY-snapshot start, no staging HOME, no un-executed pre-positioning, no
transit omitted from the time/energy ledger, \textbf{no CEM/teacher at deployment}, and never report one planner's failure
as a physical impossibility. No RL run before its coordinate + reward + demo bank are frozen and its gate passes.

\section{Frozen positive controls (R11.0 / R11.1 --- do not modify)}
\begin{itemize}[nosep,leftmargin=1.4em]
  \item \textbf{R11.0} \code{EXACT\_ZERO\_HOME\_TO\_STRICT\_K6\_BASELINE\_PASS}: zero home $\to$ 3-shell IK reach $\to$
  READY-specific CEM capture $\to$ frozen downstream $\to$ strict K6 (11.54\,mm). CEM is a \emph{teacher/oracle}, not
  deployment. The only learned component in this chain is the frozen R2 residual.
  \item \textbf{R11.1} \code{ZERO\_HOME\_RRT\_REACH\_GRID\_PASS\_WITH\_CLEARANCE\_TIGHTENING\_PENDING}: 4-DOF RRT-Connect,
  goal set of collision-free precontact configs, inflated-coin + inter-arm collision checks. Residual: $\sim$2--4\,mm
  precontact nudge in a few executions (clearance tightening), and \code{right +5\,cm} is an invalid initial condition.
  \item Frozen and reused (never the task start): the downstream (HANDOFF\_RESET/R2/coast), physics, strict-K6 + safety
  monitors, the collision contract. \code{s4}/\code{s7} untouched; \code{f1--f4} sealed.
  \item \textbf{Two components, two roles --- do not conflate.} \textbf{RRT-Connect is a certified, deterministic,
  \emph{deployed} geometric planner} (M1$\to$M2): it also serves as positive control, fallback, and an optional
  demonstration source, and is \emph{legitimately present at final deployment} --- the RL never has to relearn what the
  geometric planner solves robustly in $\sim$0.5\,s. Only the \textbf{per-instance CEM capture/delivery solve is
  teacher-only} (demonstration generator, prohibited at final deployment). \emph{Teacher-free} therefore means:
  \textbf{no per-instance CEM / oracle / precomputed delivery orbit} --- it does \emph{not} mean ``no deterministic RRT.''
\end{itemize}

\section{Task contract (first-class, verifiable)}
\textbf{Initial condition} \code{EXACT\_ZERO\_HOME\_V1}: $q{=}[0,0,0,0]$, $\dot q{=}0$, \code{prev\_tau}$=0$, empty
controller memory, empty contact set, coin velocity $0$, \code{sim.step}$=0$, no snapshot parent, no teacher state.
Coin/target poses are a \emph{separate} init distribution, not part of HOME. \textbf{Every rollout} emits an
\code{INITIAL\_CONDITION\_CERTIFICATE} (exact $q$/$\dot q$, zero memory, empty contacts, zero object velocity, step $0$, no
injection/staging); a \code{ZERO\_HOME$\to$K6} claim is valid only with a passing certificate, over one continuous episode.

\section{Hybrid system + energy model}
\textbf{Modes} $M_0$ ZERO\_HOME, $M_1$ FREE\_REACH, $M_2$ PRECONTACT\_ALIGNMENT, $M_3$ CAPTURE, $M_4$
CONTROLLED\_DELIVERY, $M_5$ TARGET\_ENTRY, $M_6$ SETTLE, $M_7$ K6\_SUCCESS. \textbf{Transitions} carry
$h_i=(q,\dot q,\tau_{\mathrm{prev}},x_c,\dot x_c,x_g,\mathcal C,e_{\mathrm{phase}},m_i,m_{i+1})$ and an
\code{EnergyTransitionCertificate}. \textbf{Per-mode Hamiltonian}
$H_m(z)=T_m+V_m+E_{\mathrm{contact},m}$ with port-Hamiltonian dynamics
$\dot z=(J_m(z)-R_m(z))\nabla H_m(z)+G_m(z)u$ ($J_m{=}{-}J_m^\top$ energy-conserving, $R_m\succeq0$ dissipation). Reset
$z^+=\Delta_{m\to n}(z^-)$; energy jump $\Delta H_{m\to n}=H_n(z^+)-H_m(z^-)$; episode ledger
$H(T)-H(0)=W_{\mathrm{act}}+W_{\mathrm{ext}}-E_{\mathrm{diss}}-E_{\mathrm{impact}}+\varepsilon_{\mathrm{num}}$. Positive and
negative actuator work $W_\pm=\int\sum_i\max(\pm\tau_i\dot q_i,0)\,dt$ are tracked \emph{separately} (they must not cancel).

\textbf{Two-level energy contract --- do not claim conservation yet.} Under MuJoCo contact, damping, actuator governance,
and numerical integration, not every energy term is exactly recoverable. R11.2 delivers only the \emph{measurement}
level: a \code{MeasuredEnergyLedger} per mode/transition (robot $E_k$, coin $E_k$, PE where relevant, $W_+$, $W_-$,
contact impulse, damping/dissipation proxy, pre- and post-transition energy, numerical residual). The R11.2
\code{EnergyTransitionCertificate} asserts \gate{ENERGY\_LEDGER\_COMPLETE} + \gate{ENERGY\_BALANCE\_RESIDUAL\_RECORDED} ---
never \gate{HAMILTONIAN\_CONSERVATION\_PASS}. The \emph{modelled} level (\code{ModelledHamiltonianCertificate}: which
residual band is physically/numerically acceptable, and any conservation verdict) is calibrated later, at R11.8.

\section{Coin/target-conditioned delivery (the real bottleneck)}
Policy state $s_t=(q_t,\dot q_t,x_c,\dot x_c,x_g,\Delta x_{\mathrm{goal}},\mathcal C_t,h_t)$ with
$\Delta x_{\mathrm{goal}}=x_g-x_c$ (absolute pose kept --- same relative vector needs different joint strategy at different
workspace points). The policy \emph{forms} the strategy from geometry; it does not store one canonical orbit. The
\textbf{CEM teacher} re-derives the whole capture--delivery orbit per instance to build demonstrations (training only);
\textbf{deployment uses no CEM, no oracle, no per-instance lookup, no precomputed delivery orbit} --- but the deterministic
RRT reach (M1$\to$M2) \emph{is} a legitimate deployed component (see \S2). Teacher-free constrains the learned
capture/delivery, not the certified geometric planner.

\section{Distributions --- certificate-filtered, not a raw rectangle}
$\mathcal D_{\mathrm{coin}}=(\text{geometric sample region})\cap(\text{valid initial-condition set})$: a coin pose is
\emph{admissible} only if exact-zero HOME is collision-free, initial clearance $\geq5$\,mm, coin inside environment
bounds, a legal precontact goal set is nonempty, and RRT finds a certified reach within budget with precontact coin
displacement $\leq1$\,mm. \textbf{First region} about $c^\star=[0.076,0.143]$: $\Delta x\in[-0.05,+0.03]$,
$\Delta y\in[-0.04,+0.03]$\,m, \emph{after} certificate filtering. \code{right +5\,cm} is
\code{INVALID\_INITIAL\_CONDITION} --- excluded from the policy-success \emph{denominator}, reported in a separate
rejection-rate metric, never a ``hard negative.'' The current 2--4\,mm precontact nudge must first be closed with larger
obstacle inflation; only precontact-motion-gate-passing rollouts enter the demonstration bank.
\textbf{Disjoint grids (no interpolation leakage, [[held-out panel is single-use]]):} \emph{train} $\Delta x\in\{-4,-2,0,
+2\}$, $\Delta y\in\{-3,-1,+1,+3\}$\,cm (admissible pairs); \emph{dev} the half-shifted interior $\Delta x\in\{-3,-1,+1,
+3\}$, $\Delta y\in\{-2,0,+2\}$\,cm (train-excluded); \emph{final} edge-near admissible points, diagonals, continuous
random cell-interior samples, plus a separate invalid-initial-condition rejection panel.
\textbf{Target = relative goal vector, staged generalization.} \textbf{G0} translation control
($x_c'=x_c+\delta,\ x_g'=x_g+\delta$; relative delivery vector fixed --- tests transfer to another absolute workspace
point). \textbf{G1} target-direction ($\Delta x_g=r[\cos\alpha,\sin\alpha]$, $r\in[0.05,0.09]$\,m,
$\alpha\in[\alpha^\star-45^\circ,\alpha^\star+45^\circ]$). \textbf{G2} full coin$\times$direction$\times$distance
(only after G0/G1; each pair checked: target in bounds, no invalid overlap, a physical delivery path exists, the strict-K6
region fits). \textbf{R11.3 curriculum} C0 canonical/canonical $\to$ C1 shifted-together $\to$ C2 varied coin + fixed
relative goal $\to$ C3 fixed coin + varied target dir/dist $\to$ C4 varied$\times$varied $\to$ C5 unseen continuous panel
--- far more diagnostic than one large 2-D product distribution.

\section{Optimization objective (lexicographic)}
\textbf{Hard:} valid initial-condition certificate; collision/safety contract; no forbidden precontact coin motion;
strict K6; teacher-free deployment. \textbf{Secondary} (over valid-K6 episodes):
$J=\lambda_T T+\lambda_L L+\lambda_J J_{\mathrm{jerk}}+\lambda_W W_+ +\lambda_D E_{\mathrm{diss}}+\lambda_I
E_{\mathrm{impact}}+\lambda_H J_{\Delta H}+\lambda_S T_{\mathrm{settle}}$, with $J_{\Delta H}=\sum_i\psi_i(\Delta
H_i,W_{\mathrm{act},i},E_{\mathrm{impact},i})$ and $\psi_i$ asymmetric (heavily penalize unexplained positive energy
injection, penalize needless impact loss, permit physically-expected dissipation, reward hitting the mode-wise energy
window). Constrained: $\min_\pi J$ s.t. $P(\mathrm{K6})\geq P(\mathrm{baseline})-\varepsilon$,
$N_{\mathrm{safety}}=N_{\mathrm{invalid\ init}}=N_{\mathrm{premature\ contact}}=0$.

\section{Test / performance / risk (\S3, \S4, program-level)}
\textbf{Tests} at every boundary: unit (validators, invariants, metrics, energy ledger balance), integration (one
continuous zero-home episode), and --- for RL boundaries --- a 1-seed production-scale smoke before any multi-seed queue.
\textbf{Determinism:} fixed seeds; RRT seed pinned; energy ledger reproducible. \textbf{Perf:} demo-bank + RL run under the
16\,GB RSS cap, checkpointed per seed; wall estimate reconciled ($>2\times$ rule) before any overnight run.
\textbf{Risk:} (i) delivery may not generalize even with conditioning (the R10.2/knife-edge precedent) --- gated by
\gate{...GENERALIZATION\_PASS}, else report \code{NO\_GENERALIZATION\_WITHIN\_BUDGET}, never impossibility; (ii)
energy-shaping could trade K6 for efficiency --- the lexicographic hard-constraint on K6/safety forbids it; (iii) demo
grids must be disjoint (train/dev/final) to avoid leakage ([[held-out panel is single-use]]).
\textbf{Deployment guard:} deploy only if IC certificate valid, nominal K6 preserved, generalization $\geq$ baseline, zero
safety regression, bounded precontact motion + energy injection, no unexplained transition energy jump, no CEM/teacher
call, action in the certified envelope; else \code{DEPLOYMENT\_FALLBACK\_TO\_RRT\_PLUS\_CERTIFIED\_SCAFFOLD} (also from
exact zero).

\section{Rollback + hygiene}
New \code{hymeko\_ir/} + \code{hybrid\_rl/} packages and coin-adapter files; rollback $=$ delete. The IR wiring into the
reach modules is additive (certificate + provenance emission), revertible without behavior change. Each RL checkpoint saved
per seed; a regressing policy falls back to the RRT+certified-scaffold baseline. If R11 is abandoned before R11.2 lands,
delete \code{docs/plans/2026-07-29-r11-generalized-hybrid-delivery/} this session.

\section{Stop logic}
Each numbered phase is a boundary: implement, verify its gate, write the report, and \halt{} for review before the next.
No SAC/PPO/TD3 run before its boundary's coordinate + reward + demo bank are frozen and its ranking/coverage gate passes.
\textbf{This plan itself is the R11 boundary-0 artifact: no R11.2 code begins until it is approved.}
