\begin{abstract}
\noindent Stage 1B proved a structured phase-shaped moving-precursor capture delivers strict K6 from \code{HOME\_V1}
(3/3 seeds, frozen downstream). This RL is genuinely justified: not re-learning known kinematics, but turning a
\emph{proven, reachable} moving contact-handoff into a state-dependent, \emph{robust} capture policy. The critical
scientific rule: a TD3 that merely reproduces the canonical-start CEM profile proves nothing (the scaffold already
solves it). The claim is \code{TD3\_IMPROVES\_OVER\_FROZEN\_SCAFFOLD\_PASS} --- the learned policy must beat both the
frozen scaffold \emph{and} a BC-only checkpoint on a \emph{pre-registered} perturbation panel (small admissible READY /
coin-state / actuator-state variations), with no safety regression. We freeze one scaffold \code{$\pi_0$} by an
\emph{outcome-independent} rule (structural-parameter medoid), wrap it action-preservingly ($u=\mathrm{clip}(u_{\pi_0}(\phi)
+\alpha\tanh\delta_\theta(s),-1,1)$; zero residual $\equiv\pi_0$, bit-exact, tested before training), and train a genuine
reward-driven TD3 residual over only READY$\to$capture$\to$REACH\_TO\_APPROACH --- everything else frozen. \emph{Learn the
recovery, not the trajectory.}
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope and goal}
\textbf{Goal.} A learned TD3 residual capture policy, deployed with no CEM/teacher in the loop, that (i) delivers
teacher-free canonical \code{HOME\_V1}$\to$strict K6 and (ii) \emph{improves robustness} over the frozen scaffold and
BC-only on a pre-registered development perturbation panel. \textbf{Deployed chain:}
\begin{center}\itshape HOME\_V1 $\to$ frozen analytic transit $\to$ learned TD3 dynamic capture $\to$ frozen APPROACH
$\to$ HANDOFF\_RESET $\to$ frozen R2 $\to$ strict K6.\end{center}
\textbf{Non-goals (explicit stops).} No full multiseed panel; no SAC/PPO comparison; no Hamiltonian/energy/entropy
experiments. No modification of the frozen downstream, the analytic transit, or physics/safety/K6. This is the bounded
first campaign only.

\section{Test strategy}
\textbf{Unit:} medoid selection determinism + outcome-independence; \code{capture\_observation} schema + no future/teacher
leak; reward monotonicity (K6 dominates shaping); panel determinism. \textbf{Integration/identity (load-bearing):}
\code{RESIDUAL\_ZERO\_IS\_SCAFFOLD\_IDENTITY} --- zero residual reproduces $\pi_0$'s rollout \emph{bit-exact}; the wrapped
scaffold at $\alpha{=}0$ delivers the same K6/min\_dtz as \code{moving\_precapture}. \textbf{Smoke (production scale, 1
seed):} a short TD3 run improves panel K6 over BC-only/scaffold (else \halt{} and localise --- reward, obs, or exploration).
\textbf{Boundary:} exactly-one READY$\to$CAPTURE / REACH\_TO\_APPROACH / HANDOFF\_RESET; deterministic replay.
\textbf{Coverage:} every new function exercised.

\section{Performance budget}
One capture episode + downstream $\approx 0.1$\,s; \code{total\_options}$\le600$/seed, eval every 25; 3 seeds
$\approx$ 10--20\,min, \emph{checkpointed per seed}. \textbf{Peak RSS $<2$\,GB} (torch actor/critic; hard cap 16\,GB).
Unit suite $<180$\,s. The run reports peak RSS + wall on exit; wall-estimate reconciled before launch (per the $>2\times$
halt rule).

\section{Risk anticipation}
\textbf{Performance-contract preservation.} The residual is bounded ($\alpha\tanh$, then the clip to $[-1,1]$ and the
$\times$slew$\to$governor); it cannot exceed the slew/torque/joint-speed caps. Zero residual is bit-exact $\pi_0$, so the
worst case is never worse than the proven scaffold. \textbf{The scientific trap.} Canonical-start K6 is \emph{necessary
but not sufficient} --- $\pi_0$ already passes it. The reward-driven claim rides entirely on the pre-registered
perturbation panel and the 3-way (scaffold / BC-only / post-TD3) comparison; the panel + selection rule are fixed
\emph{before} any TD3 run and never tuned to results. \textbf{Reward hygiene.} Terminal strict-K6 dominates the bounded
shaping so the policy cannot farm a ``good handoff score / bad downstream'' side-path (the failure mode seen in Stage 1B
coordinate-matching). \textbf{Worst-case input:} admissible READY tracking error + coin-state + actuator-state
perturbations (the panel) --- the regime where the fixed scaffold is expected to degrade and a state-dependent policy
should recover.

\section{Rollback path}
New files only; rollback $=$ delete. Zero-residual identity guarantees a revert to $\pi_0$ (\code{d6974b95}) with no
behaviour change. Each checkpoint (\code{update0}/BC/best) is saved; a bad post-TD3 reverts to BC-only or scaffold.

\section{Gate ladder + stop logic}
\begin{enumerate}[nosep,leftmargin=1.6em]
  \item \code{RESIDUAL\_ZERO\_IS\_SCAFFOLD\_IDENTITY\_PASS} (before training).
  \item \code{HOME\_V1\_START\_STRICT\_K6\_PASS}: post-TD3, teacher-free canonical HOME$\to$K6 (necessary).
  \item \code{TD3\_CAPTURE\_REWARD\_DRIVEN\_PASS}: reward-driven improvement over the BC-only checkpoint.
  \item \code{TD3\_IMPROVES\_OVER\_FROZEN\_SCAFFOLD\_PASS}: post-TD3 beats \emph{both} $\pi_0$ and BC-only on the
  pre-registered panel (higher K6 rate / better handoff margin), no safety regression --- \emph{the} learning claim.
\end{enumerate}
\textbf{Stop logic.} Freeze the \emph{first} checkpoint meeting all of: teacher-free canonical K6; improvement over
BC-only; improvement over scaffold on the panel; no safety/stall/clamp/reversal; exactly-one each boundary transition;
deterministic replay. Then commit and \halt{} --- no full multiseed, no SAC/PPO, no energy experiments. If the panel
improvement fails, \halt{} and localise (reward shaping, observation, exploration), do \emph{not} widen scope.
