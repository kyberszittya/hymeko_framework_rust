\begin{abstract}
\noindent The stricter compositional benchmark starts the arms at a collision-free home posture and asks the frozen
learned stack to deliver strict K6. The audit (\code{HOME\_START\_COMPOSITION\_NEEDS\_UPSTREAM\_REACH\_SKILL}) showed the
frozen \code{APPROACH} has no reach-to-contact capability, so a \emph{single} upstream skill is missing:
\code{HOME}$\rightarrow$pre-capture \code{READY}. The key realisation of this plan is that for a \textbf{planar 2R arm this
upstream skill is not a learning problem at all} --- the inverse kinematics has an exact closed form (Siciliano), with
exactly two elbow branches selected by the sign of $q_2$. We therefore build the reach as an \textbf{object-generic
Geometric Approach layer}: object geometry $\rightarrow$ approach side $\rightarrow$ pre-contact tip targets
$p^\star=p_{\text{contact}}-d_{\text{pre}}\,\hat n$ $\rightarrow$ analytic branch-continuous IK along a collision-free
tip-space path $\rightarrow$ \code{READY}, time-parameterised under the frozen slew/torque contract. Learning is reserved
for what kinematics cannot cover --- the contact-rich \emph{dynamic capture} (velocity-matched straddle for a coin) and
the existing R2 transport. A learning-free positive control is already in hand: from the retained generic home
\code{V1} the analytic transit reaches the pre-capture shell with \textbf{coin perturbation $0.0$\,mm, zero contact,
single elbow branch, slew-feasible (max joint step $0.069\ll0.300$)}. This plan formalises that probe into an audited,
tested module and its contracts. \emph{Kinematics where kinematics suffices; learning only at the contact.}
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope and goal}
\textbf{Goal.} A deterministic, auditable \code{HOME}$\rightarrow$\code{READY} transit for the two-arm planar gripper,
built entirely from explicit closed-form 2R IK + a collision-free tip-space path, with \emph{no RL and no BC}.
\textbf{Architecture (the reusable layer boundary):}
\begin{center}\itshape GEOMETRIC APPROACH (explicit, this change) $\rightarrow$ READY / pre-contact manifold
$\rightarrow$ learned local contact skill (dynamic capture) $\rightarrow$ frozen APPROACH $\rightarrow$ HANDOFF\_RESET
$\rightarrow$ R2 $\rightarrow$ K6.\end{center}
\textbf{Non-goals (explicit stop conditions).} No RL and no BC in this change (they are justified only for amortising the
planner across many geometries/obstacles, which the current fixed planar problem does not need --- analytic IK is more
precise, simpler, and auditable). No modification of the frozen downstream (APPROACH / HANDOFF\_RESET / R2 / coast / K6
/ physics / safety). No dynamic-capture CEM and no TD3 in this change --- they are the \emph{next} milestones, committed
separately.

\section{Interface changes (signatures, contracts)}
All additive; no existing signature changes.
\begin{itemize}[nosep,leftmargin=1.4em]
  \item \code{PlanarArm2R(shoulder\_dof, elbow\_dof, base, tip\_geom, l1, l2)} with \code{calibrate(model, snapshot)}
  measuring $(\theta_b,s_0,o_1,s_1)$ from FK geometry. \code{ik(tip, branch)$\to$(a,b)} exact closed form; \code{ik\_cont
  (tip, prev)} picks the branch minimising $\lVert\mathrm{wrap}(q-q_{\text{prev}})\rVert$. \textbf{Pre:} \code{tip} inside
  the annulus $(\lvert l_1-l_2\rvert,\,l_1+l_2)$. \textbf{Post:} $\mathrm{FK}(\code{ik}(t))=t$ to $<10^{-3}$\,m
  (round-trip, asserted); the returned branch has constant $\mathrm{sign}(b)$ along a continuous in-annulus path.
  \item \code{ApproachTargets} protocol: \code{precontact(model, snapshot)$\to$(tipL$^\star$, tipR$^\star$,
  branchL, branchR)}. \code{CoinStraddleTargets(shell\_dist)} is the coin instance (two tips at the shell radius on the
  assigned sides). The seam is what a pyramid/prism instance would replace --- \emph{no} coin logic elsewhere.
  \item \code{plan\_collision\_free\_transit(home, targets, arm\_L, arm\_R, cfg)$\to$TransitPlan} --- builds each arm's
  tip path (radial-out $\to$ keep-out arc $\to$ radial-in to the assigned side), solves branch-continuous IK per
  waypoint, \textbf{validates every interpolated state} (tip$\to$coin keep-out, link$\to$coin clearance, in-annulus /
  non-singular $r_{\text{base}}<r_{\max}$, joint limits, per-step $\le$ slew), and returns a densified, slew-feasible
  \code{q\_ref} trajectory. On a per-arm infeasibility it retries the alternative arc direction, then falls back to
  \emph{sequential} (right-to-shell then left) execution; it never silently truncates --- an unroutable request raises
  \code{TransitInfeasible} with the failing waypoint/reason.
  \item \code{GeometricTransitController.roll(home)$\to$TransitResult} --- governed-servo tracking of \code{q\_ref};
  reports reached-shell error, coin perturbation, min tip$\to$coin clearance, contact count, peak qvel, and the terminal
  \code{TransportSnapshot} that the READY $\to$ capture boundary consumes. \textbf{Post:} deterministic given
  \code{(home, plan)}.
\end{itemize}

\section{Test strategy (per layer)}
\textbf{Unit} (\code{test\_planar\_geometric\_approach.py}, deterministic seeds): calibration recovers
$(\theta_b,s_0,o_1,s_1)$ and round-trips V1/V2/shell to $0$\,mm; \code{ik\_cont} maintains elbow sign across a fine arc
(a regression that would fail for a naive numerical DLS that hops branches); \code{ik} raises/clamps outside the annulus;
\code{plan\_collision\_free\_transit} \emph{rejects} a path forced over the unreachable far side (\code{TransitInfeasible})
and \emph{accepts} the near-base route; the produced \code{q\_ref} has max step $<$ slew; \code{CoinStraddleTargets} puts
the two targets on opposite assigned sides at the shell radius.
\textbf{Integration:} the full V1$\to$READY roll satisfies the four transit gates below on the canonical s1 coin.
\textbf{Regression:} a coarse (corner-cutting) path must fail \code{COIN\_UNPERTURBED} (the 100\,mm-knock failure mode we
measured), proving the densification/validation is load-bearing.
\textbf{Boundary:} \code{REACH\_TO\_APPROACH\_ONE\_STEP\_IDENTITY} --- the terminal READY snapshot, advanced one online
step, equals the offline READY state (no double phase-machine invocation, the boundary lesson); and an off-by-one
mis-timed boundary is \emph{rejected}.
\textbf{Coverage:} every new public/private function exercised by $\ge1$ new test.

\section{Performance budget}
\textbf{Measured anchors (this host, Apple-Silicon, \code{.venv} py3.11.15, mujoco 3.10.0):} the full transit probe (151
waypoints $\times$ 3 substeps + hold) runs in $<1$\,s at RSS $<0.3$\,GB; calibration is $\sim$8 FK evals. \textbf{Budget:}
unit+integration+boundary suite $<90$\,s wall; \textbf{peak RSS $<1$\,GB} (hard cap 16\,GB, never approached). The perf
test asserts a numerical wall bound ($<3$\,s median over 5 iterations) and RSS bound on the transit roll.

\section{Risk anticipation}
\textbf{Performance-contract preservation.} The transit adds \emph{no} new torque authority: \code{q\_ref} is tracked by
the \emph{same} \code{step\_ablation}$\to$\code{govern\_torque} stack the rest of the system uses, so the slew, torque,
and joint/coin-speed caps are inherited unchanged. The densification guarantees per-step $\le$ slew \emph{before}
tracking, so the servo never has to cut a corner into the coin.
\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Worst-case input / production scale.} The load-bearing geometric fact: the coin centre sits
$0.271$\,m from each arm base while the arm reach is $l_1+l_2=0.30$\,m, so the coin's \emph{far} edge
($0.271+r_{\text{keepout}}\approx0.316>0.30$) is \emph{unreachable}. The left tip therefore \emph{must} route the
near-base way (dir $=-1$); the naive over-the-top arc drives IK onto the singular circle ($r_{\text{base}}\to0.30$,
$c\to\pm1$) and the tracker then knocks the coin ($\sim$100\,mm, measured). \textbf{Mitigation:} the planner validates
$r_{\text{base}}<r_{\max}=0.298$ at every waypoint and rejects the far-side route, and the near-base arc is verified to
stay non-singular (elbow bend $\gtrsim20^\circ$ at $r_{\text{base}}=0.291$). The near-base swing must also clear the
opposite arm and its links (self-collision check per waypoint) --- the sequential fallback exists precisely for the case
where the two arms' simultaneous near-base swings would interfere.
\smallskip\noindent\textbf{V2 singularity note.} \code{HOME\_STATE\_V2\_READY}'s left tip sits at $r_{\text{base}}=0.300$
(exactly full extension, $q_1\approx0$). V2 is used as a \emph{ready-set target region}, not a hard pose to track through
the singular point; the transit approaches READY \emph{radially inward} (away from the singularity), and the READY guard
tolerances (not a single pose) absorb the last millimetres.

\section{Gate ladder}
\begin{enumerate}[nosep,leftmargin=1.6em]
  \item \code{SINGLE\_BRANCH\_MAINTAINED\_PASS}: the analytic IK holds one elbow sign per arm across the whole path
  (no topological switch; the far side is unreachable so the near-base branch is forced and consistent).
  \item \code{COIN\_UNPERTURBED\_DURING\_TRANSIT\_PASS}: coin displacement $\le\varepsilon$ ($\approx1$\,mm) and zero
  fingertip contact across the roll.
  \item \code{TRANSIT\_REACHES\_READY\_PASS}: both tips arrive inside the READY / pre-capture band (shell radius
  $\pm$ tol), qvel small.
  \item \code{HOME\_V1\_TO\_READY\_COLLISION\_FREE\_PASS} \emph{(this change's headline gate)}: all of the above hold on
  the retained generic \code{V1}, with per-waypoint validation and no state edit.
  \item \code{REACH\_TO\_APPROACH\_ONE\_STEP\_IDENTITY\_PASS} \emph{(boundary)}: offline READY $=$ one online step from
  READY; an off-by-one is rejected.
  \item \emph{(Next milestones, not this change)} \code{HOME\_V1\_TO\_DYNAMIC\_HANDOFF\_REACHABILITY\_PASS} ($\ge3$
  planner seeds, short capture CEM from READY reaches $H_{\text{dyn}}$), then \code{HOME\_V1\_START\_STRICT\_K6\_PASS}
  (TD3 finishes; teacher-free). If the capture reaches $H_{\text{dyn}}$ but K6 fails, \halt{} and correct the certificate
  --- do not tune the frozen downstream.
\end{enumerate}
