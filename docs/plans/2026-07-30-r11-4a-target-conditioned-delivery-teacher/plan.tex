\begin{abstract}
\noindent R11.3 proved the demonstration \emph{infrastructure} (exact-zero certificates, deployed RRT reach, valid
precontact handoffs, real target relocation) and localized the blocker: the \textbf{frozen canonical downstream (R2) does
not generalize} --- 8/64 strict-K6, failures concentrated in \emph{delivery} (21) and \emph{target-entry/settle} (33
scenario-best; 68 at the all-attempt level) with \textbf{0 \code{precontact\_handoff\_invalid}}. A capture-phase proxy is
\emph{suggestive} (successes have lower terminal velocity scale) but \textbf{unproven} without delivery/settle energy.
R11.4A replaces the frozen R2 delivery with a \textbf{target-conditioned delivery+settle teacher}: a per-scenario CEM over
the structured \code{forward\_displacement.rollout\_primitive} (deliver mode), which already pushes/brakes/releases/settles
the coin toward \code{direction\_to\_zone} (the relocatable target) and monitors the frozen K6 certificate. R11.4A also
extends \code{MeasuredEnergyLedger} across capture$\to$delivery$\to$target-entry$\to$settle so the energy-surplus mechanism
becomes a \emph{measured} claim. \textbf{Measure before optimizing; promote PLAUSIBLE$\to$SUPPORTED only under matched
scenarios.} A bounded pilot (12 settle + 8 delivery + 2 control), then \halt{}. Verdict:
\gate{R11\_4A\_TARGET\_CONDITIONED\_DELIVERY\_TEACHER\_PILOT\_PASS} or
\gate{R11\_4A\_PIPELINE\_PASS\_TEACHER\_RECOVERY\_INSUFFICIENT}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Binding review refinements}
\textbf{(1) Prove target-conditioning, pre-pilot.} Contract tests: relocating the target changes
\code{direction\_to\_zone}; rotating it rotates the primary push; braking + the K6 monitor use the \emph{same} relocated
zone; no hidden canonical target / absolute R2 reference; the relocated target is the rollout's \emph{terminal} goal, not
a report field. Gates \gate{TARGET\_RELOCATION\_PROPAGATES\_THROUGH\_DELIVERY\_PASS},
\gate{NO\_CANONICAL\_DELIVERY\_ORBIT\_DEPENDENCY\_PASS}. \textbf{(2) Phase A opens dimensions incrementally:} freeze the
early push \{squeeze, forward, balance\}; the CEM searches \emph{only} \{brake onset (\code{ramp}), brake gain, release
timing, entry-speed target, settle damping, settle dwell, rebound suppression\}. Only if that is insufficient open
\{forward magnitude, delivery duration, direction correction, balance, ramp\}. This distinguishes energy-shedding from
"needed a new orbit." \textbf{(3) Energy is NOT an optimization objective in this pilot} --- the CEM is driven by
K6/safety/progress/overshoot (the existing \code{score}); the energy ledger is observer/diagnostic only, so a lower
target-entry energy in the winners is \emph{discovered}, not fitted. Energy becomes an explicit objective no earlier than
R11.8. \textbf{(4) Instrumentation proven non-invasive:} the phase hook/energy read must not change control-call order,
physics-step count, event timing, RNG consumption, or the trajectory. Gate
\gate{ENERGY\_INSTRUMENTATION\_NONINVASIVE\_PASS} --- same seed+params, hook vs. no-hook rollout bit-exact (or within a
pre-declared tolerance). (The \code{rollout\_primitive} \code{frame\_hook} is contractually non-behavioural; this gate
proves it.) \textbf{(5) Full provenance per attempt:} coin/target geometry, relative goal vector, capture outcome,
delivery + settle params, phase-event timestamps, target-entry state, energy ledger, K6 result, failure class, teacher
seed, provenance hash --- BC needs the geometry/handoff/energy context, not just the actions. \textbf{Settle-extension
coordinate} (separate block, normalized + scenario-relative): \code{entry\_speed\_target}, \code{brake\_onset\_distance}
(fraction of remaining delivery distance, not absolute mm), \code{settle\_damping}, \code{settle\_dwell},
\code{rebound\_gain}. \textbf{Pilot selection}: the 12 settle-failures span C0--C3, short + long delivery distances,
differing target directions, and low + high capture \code{vel\_scale} --- not one easy settle cured twelve times.

\section{Scope, non-goals}
\textbf{Goal.} A per-instance target-conditioned delivery+settle solver that re-derives the post-capture trajectory from
the current coin pose/velocity, target pose, relative goal vector, robot + contact state, and handoff descriptor --- never
reusing a frozen canonical delivery orbit as the executable solution. \textbf{Non-goals (hard):} no BC, no SAC/TD3/PPO, no
policy assimilation, no reach refinement, no Hamiltonian reward shaping. \textbf{Roles unchanged:} RRT deployed; every
CEM/search call is a labelled training teacher in provenance; no teacher-free label. \textbf{Two-level energy contract
preserved:} measured ledger + residuals only; \code{conservation\_verdict()} continues to refuse
(\gate{HAMILTONIAN\_CONSERVATION\_PASS} is R11.8).

\section{Established (R11.3) --- do not overclaim}
Exact-zero certificates work; deployed RRT reach generalizes over the admissible coin region; precontact handoffs valid;
target relocation real + correctly represented (C3 \code{entry\_dtz} 50--90\,mm matches r=5--9\,cm); failures localize to
delivery + target-entry/settle; the frozen downstream does not generalize. The capture-phase proxy (successes
\code{vel\_scale} 1.56 vs settle-failures 1.80; reach work + capture direction flat) \textbf{supports but does not prove}:
\emph{excess/poorly-directed energy at target entry contributes to delivery/settle failure.} \textbf{Not a confirmed
mechanism until delivery+settle energy is measured directly.}

\section{Teacher pipeline}
\begin{center}\itshape EXACT\_ZERO\_HOME $\to$ deployed RRT reach $\to$ precontact handoff $\to$ capture solve $\to$
\textbf{target-conditioned delivery solve $\to$ target-entry braking $\to$ release / contact-mode $\to$ settle solve}
$\to$ strict K6.\end{center}
The delivery+settle stages are re-derived per instance. \textbf{Grounding:} the executable primitive is the existing
\code{forward\_displacement.rollout\_primitive} in \code{deliver} mode --- its PUSH/BRAKE/RELEASE schedule already aims at
\code{direction\_to\_zone} (relocatable target), brakes on measured coin speed, and monitors the frozen K6 certificate
(\code{dtz}$\le$CENTER\_TOL $\wedge$ speed$<$SETTLE\_VEL, HELD\_DWELL, touched). R11.4A wraps it in a per-scenario CEM
search and \textbf{replaces \code{FrozenDownstream.deliver} (R2)} with it. See \code{plan.tikz}.

\section{Searchable teacher parameters}
A structured coordinate (bounded, inside safety/torque/slew/collision/contact contracts), mapping onto the primitive +
settle extension: \textbf{delivery direction} (target-conditioned push via \code{e\_par}=$\hat u_{\mathrm{zone}}$, lateral
balance correction, goal-vector alignment); \textbf{magnitude/duration} (forward force/torque scale, transport duration /
ramp, velocity target, acceleration profile); \textbf{target-entry braking} (braking onset by remaining distance, brake
gain, desired entry speed, velocity-feedback term); \textbf{release/contact} (release timing, preload reduction,
contact-mode continuation vs. switch, optional guided coast); \textbf{settle} (terminal-velocity target, settle damping,
dwell duration, rebound suppression, strict-K6 dwell verification). No raw per-step torques.

\section{Delivery + settle energy instrumentation}
Extend \code{MeasuredEnergyLedger} across capture / delivery / target entry / settle (phase-marked frame hook on the
rollout). Record per phase: robot $E_k$; coin translational $E_k$; coin rotational $E_k$ (if available); $W_+$; $W_-$;
contact impulse; impact-energy proxy; dissipation proxy; and energy \emph{after capture}, \emph{at target entry}, \emph{at
braking onset}, \emph{at release}, \emph{at settle start}, \emph{at K6 / terminal failure}; numerical residual. Derived:
$T_{\mathrm{coin,entry}}$, $H_{\mathrm{entry}}$, $\Delta H_{\mathrm{capture}\to\mathrm{entry}}$,
$\Delta H_{\mathrm{entry}\to\mathrm{settle}}$, and target-directed-coin-energy $/\,W_+$. The certificate still asserts only
\gate{ENERGY\_LEDGER\_COMPLETE}/\gate{ENERGY\_BALANCE\_RESIDUAL\_RECORDED}; \code{conservation\_verdict()} refuses.

\section{Recovery sequence}
\textbf{Phase A --- settle failures first} (cheapest recoverable: coin already in the vicinity). Search braking onset,
target-entry speed, release timing, damping, dwell, rebound suppression. Compare frozen canonical downstream vs.
target-conditioned teacher; measure recovery-to-K6, $T_{\mathrm{coin,entry}}$, overshoot, rebound, settle time, actuator
work, safety. \textbf{Phase B --- delivery failures} (after settle recovery verified): search push direction, transport
duration, force/torque magnitude, mid-course correction, braking profile. \textbf{Phase C --- capture failures}
(deprioritized; kept a distinct class; not until delivery+settle coverage improves).

\section{Matched mechanism analysis}
No pooled energy comparisons. Compare the \emph{same} coin/target scenario across teacher variants, within matched
delivery-distance bands, matched target-direction bands, matched initial handoff quality. The energy-surplus hypothesis is
promoted PLAUSIBLE$\to$SUPPORTED \emph{only if} recovery is associated --- within matched scenarios --- with reduced
$T_{\mathrm{coin,entry}}$ (or improved target-directed energy ratio), reduced overshoot/rebound, improved strict-K6, and no
safety regression.

\section{Pilot scope + gates}
\textbf{Scope:} $\sim$12 scenario-best settle failures + 8 delivery failures + 2 success controls (canonical/translated).
Per scenario: retain the frozen baseline; run $\le 5$ teacher seeds / search restarts; retain \emph{every} attempt; never
overwrite failures with later successes; link all to provenance hashes. Bounded search only. \textbf{Gates:} exact-zero
certificate preserved; RRT deployed; all teacher calls labelled in provenance; delivery uses the actual coin+target
geometry; target-entry + settle energy ledgers complete; no conservation claim; failure classes distinct; serialization +
replay pass; no safety/boundary regression. \textbf{Scientific recovery gate:} $\ge 50\%$ of selected settle-failure
scenarios recover to strict K6. \textbf{Diagnostic delivery gate:} $\ge 1$ previously-failed noncanonical delivery
scenario recovers. \textbf{Verdict:} \gate{R11\_4A\_TARGET\_CONDITIONED\_DELIVERY\_TEACHER\_PILOT\_PASS}; if infra passes
but recovery does not, \gate{R11\_4A\_PIPELINE\_PASS\_TEACHER\_RECOVERY\_INSUFFICIENT} --- stop + report honestly.

\section{Full coverage target (after pilot PASS only)}
Extend across the 64-scenario bank; require $\ge 70\%$ admissible scenarios with $\ge 1$ strict-K6; $\ge 50\%$ positive
coverage in each C0--C3 stage; positives across multiple coin+target regions; disjoint train/dev/test positives; no
geometric region represented by a single canonical orbit. Verdict
\gate{TARGET\_CONDITIONED\_DELIVERY\_TEACHER\_COVERAGE\_PASS}. If it fails, do not start BC.

\section{Affected files (new; from \code{5b3995e1}; no CORE.YAML items)}

\section{Stop logic}
Production-scale smoke (1 scenario) before any queue; reconcile the wall estimate ($>2\times$ rule). Run the bounded pilot,
verify the gates, write the pilot report, commit only then, push fast-forward, and \halt{} for review. \textbf{No R11.4B
BC or R11.5 RL until the full-coverage gate passes.} \emph{Measure delivery+settle energy before optimizing it.}
