\begin{abstract}
\noindent Stage 1B proved a phase-shaped moving-precursor capture delivers strict K6 from \code{HOME\_V1} (3/3 seeds,
frozen downstream). The Stage-2 audit (\code{4d983d4f}) then established a \emph{negative-first} result: the previous
action coordinate --- a per-step slew-normalised residual $u=\mathrm{clip}(u_{\pi_0}(\phi)+\alpha\tanh\delta_\theta(s),\pm1)$
whose $\delta_\theta$ is re-evaluated \emph{each step} --- makes vanilla TD3 explore with per-step IID Gaussian noise that
\emph{integrates into the actuator preload} ($\delta\tau_T\!\approx\!\alpha\slew\sum_t\varepsilon_t$), removing the delivery
at every tested scale, while a temporally-\emph{coherent} correction recovers $8/8$. The fix is not to abandon TD3 but to
\textbf{move the action out of the per-step slew-increment coordinate}. The learned decision is made \emph{once, at READY},
as a bounded structured option
$\theta=(\Delta s,\Delta p,\Delta b,\;c_1,c_2\in\mathbb{R}^4,\;\Delta\tau_T\in\mathbb{R}^4)\in\mathbb{R}^{15}$;
the per-step physical action is a \emph{deterministic tracker} of the resulting smooth desired-torque path
$\tau_{\mathrm{des}}(\phi)=\tau_0^\theta(\phi)+\sum_k c_k\psi_k(\phi)+\psi_T(\phi)\,\Delta\tau_T$, so exploration is
temporally coherent by construction. \textbf{This is a coordinate-freeze plan, not yet a learning claim.} Three identity
gates are verified \emph{before any training} (zero-$\theta$ $\equiv$ medoid scaffold bit-exact on the \emph{same} code path
$\Rightarrow$ strict K6); then episodic-only exploration admissibility (incl.\ terminal-offset tracking / no preload
random-walk); only then a bounded 3-seed TD3 whose success is \emph{improvement over the frozen scaffold under
perturbation}, never nominal K6 (the scaffold already has it). No SAC/PPO until this coordinate is frozen.
\end{abstract}

\section{What changed from the retired coordinate (\code{4d983d4f} audit)}
\begin{itemize}[nosep,leftmargin=1.4em]
  \item \textbf{Decision cadence.} Retired: a residual $\delta_\theta(s)$ re-evaluated \emph{every step} (per-step action).
  New: one $\theta$ emitted \emph{once at READY} (episode-fixed); the per-step action is a fixed deterministic function of
  $\theta$ and the phase.
  \item \textbf{Exploration geometry.} Retired: \code{train\_semi\_mdp}'s Gaussian is added to the per-step residual
  $\Rightarrow$ IID slew-increment noise that random-walks the preload. New: the \emph{same} engine's per-option Gaussian is
  added to $\theta$ $\Rightarrow$ one coherent structured perturbation per episode. \emph{The coordinate change is what makes
  the reused engine admissible.}
  \item \textbf{Preload.} Retired: terminal preload is a hidden noise integral. New: preload change is the explicit,
  bounded terminal-offset block $\Delta\tau_T$ with a fixed basis $\psi_T(0){=}0,\psi_T(1){=}1$; requested-vs-executed
  offset is gated (\code{TERMINAL\_OFFSET\_TRACKING} / \code{NO\_PRELOAD\_RANDOM\_WALK}).
  \item \textbf{Reward reference.} Retired: cradle-centred $\tau^\star$ preload metric --- \emph{invalidated} (medoid
  terminal $\lVert\text{prev}-\tau^\star\rVert\!\approx\!0.88$, not $0$). New: shaping is \emph{scaffold-phase-tube}-relative
  only ($q-q_0(\phi),\ \dot q-\dot q_0(\phi),\ \text{prev}-\tau_0(\phi)$); every $\tau^\star$ term removed.
\end{itemize}

\section{The action coordinate (interface + contracts)}
Let $\pi_0$ be the frozen medoid scaffold (structural-parameter medoid of the 3 Stage-1B K6 solutions;
\code{freeze\_scaffold}), with nominal params $(s,p,b,\text{residual})$ and per-step slew-normalised action
$a_{\pi_0}(\phi)=\mathrm{clip}(\text{target}(\phi)-\text{prev},-\slew,\slew)/\slew\in[-1,1]^4$ where
$\slew=\tau_{\mathrm{rate}}\!\cdot\!\text{control\_dt}$ (\code{PhaseShapeCapture.scaffold\_action}).

\textbf{Structured option (emitted once at READY):}
$\theta=(\Delta s,\Delta p,\Delta b,c_1,c_2,\Delta\tau_T)\in\mathbb{R}^{15}$, decoded from the actor's tanh-bounded output
$\hat z\in[-1,1]^{15}$ by fixed, pre-registered per-block scales (a \code{StructuredOption} dataclass; $\hat z{=}0\Rightarrow$
all offsets exactly $0$).

\textbf{Structurally-reshaped nominal.} $\tau_0^\theta(\phi)$ is the scaffold nominal under the perturbed params
$(s{+}\Delta s,\,p{+}\Delta p,\,b{+}\Delta b)$; at $\theta{=}0$ these are bit-identical to $(s,p,b)$ (since $x+0.0=x$), so the
quintic coefficients and preload blend are bit-identical.

\textbf{Additive torque-path offset.} $o_\theta(\phi)=\sum_{k\in\{1,2\}} c_k\,\psi_k(\phi)+\psi_T(\phi)\,\Delta\tau_T$ with
transient bases $\psi_k(0){=}\psi_k(1){=}0$ (a knot spline pinned to $0$ at both endpoints --- modifies the middle, no
unnoticed endpoint drift) and terminal basis $\psi_T(0){=}0,\psi_T(1){=}1$ (a monotone ramp; the preload change is explicit
and bounded, never a noise integral).

\textbf{Deterministic per-step tracker (the key).} The desired torque path is
$\tau_{\mathrm{des}}(\phi)=\tau_0^\theta(\phi)+o_\theta(\phi)$, tracked by
\[
a_t=\mathrm{clip}\!\Big(\tfrac{\tau_{\mathrm{des}}(\phi_{t+1})-\text{prev}_t}{\slew},-1,1\Big),\qquad
\text{prev}_{t+1}=\mathrm{clip}(\text{prev}_t+\mathrm{clip}(a_t,\pm1)\,\slew,\ \text{lo},\text{hi}),
\]
a tracking form that cannot accumulate action error (it re-references the \emph{current} \code{prev}$_t$ each step). No
per-step residual noise is ever added after this conversion.

\textbf{Bit-exact realisation (float-exactness contract).} A literal $(\tau_{\mathrm{des}}-\text{prev})/\slew$ would route
$a_{\pi_0}$ through a $\slew$-divide-then-multiply round-trip and lose bit-exactness at $\theta{=}0$. Instead the tracker is
computed as
$a_t=\mathrm{clip}\big(a_{\pi_0}^\theta(\phi)+o_\theta(\phi)/\slew,\,-1,1\big)$, injecting the offset into the scaffold's
\emph{un-normalised} numerator $\mathrm{clip}(\text{target}-\text{prev},-\slew,\slew)$ via the reused
\code{scaffold\_action}/\code{apply\_step}. This is algebraically the same $\tau_{\mathrm{des}}$ tracker with
$\tau_0^\theta(\phi)=\text{prev}+\mathrm{clip}(\text{target}-\text{prev},-\slew,\slew)$, and at $\theta{=}0$ ($o_\theta\!\equiv\!0$)
reduces to $\mathrm{clip}(a_{\pi_0},-1,1)=a_{\pi_0}$ \emph{on the identical code path}.

\textbf{Contracts inherited from the scaffold (\S CLAUDE.md 2 risk):} every command passes \code{step\_ablation} $+$ governor
$+$ the final \code{clip}$\to\!\times\slew$; torque / slew / joint-speed caps and ``no state edit, no hidden force'' hold by
\emph{reuse}, not re-implementation. The one new degree of freedom is the explicit terminal preload $\Delta\tau_T$, which the
tracking-gate keeps inside the scaffold's tight-on-preload deliverable band.

\section{Reward (K6-dominant; shaping is phase-tube-relative only)}
$R=w_{K6}\,\mathbf{1}[\text{strict K6}] + w_{d}\,\rho(\text{min\_dtz}) - w_{s}\,\mathbf{1}[\lnot\text{safe}]
- w_{b}\,(\text{boundary violations}) - w_{\phi}\,\bar S_\theta$, where the \emph{only} shaping term is the bounded
phase-tube deviation $\bar S_\theta=\frac1N\sum_t \big(\lambda_q\lVert q-q_0(\phi)\rVert^2 + \lambda_v\lVert\dot q-\dot
q_0(\phi)\rVert^2 + \lambda_\tau\lVert\text{prev}-\tau_0(\phi)\rVert^2\big)$. Weights are set so
$w_{K6}\gg\sup|\text{shaping}|$: strict K6 cannot be out-farmed by tube-hugging, and the tube is shaping, never a target
(it \emph{is} the zero-$\theta$ trajectory, so hugging it $\to$ the scaffold, not an exploit). \textbf{No cradle-$\tau^\star$
term anywhere.}

($\psi_k(0){=}\psi_k(1){=}0$, $\psi_T(0){=}0,\psi_T(1){=}1$); phase-tube recorder shape/finiteness; reward K6-dominance

\code{TORQUE\_PATH\_ZERO\_DELTA\_IDENTITY} (real 15-D zero-init actor $\Rightarrow$ $\theta{=}0$ $\Rightarrow$ per-step
action bit-exact $a_{\pi_0}$), \code{TORQUE\_PATH\_BIT\_EXACT\_SCAFFOLD} (terminal qpos/qvel/prev bit-exact to
\code{PhaseShapeCapture.roll(pi0)}), \code{OPTION\_ZERO\_POLICY\_K6} (zero-$\theta$ $\to$ frozen downstream $\to$ strict K6).
\textbf{Terminal-offset tracking:} requested $\Delta\tau_T$ vs executed terminal $\text{prev}-\tau_0(1)$ agree within a
slew/contact tolerance (\code{NO\_PRELOAD\_RANDOM\_WALK}). \textbf{Smoke (production scale, 1 seed):} a short TD3 run over the
real dev-scale env (real mujoco, full downstream horizon) executes end-to-end and improves panel K6 over the zero-actor,
else \halt{} and localise (reward, obs, exploration scale). \textbf{Boundary:} exactly-one HANDOFF\_RESET; deterministic
replay given the seed. \textbf{Coverage:} every new function/method exercised; the identity gate is the regression test that
would fail against a non-bit-exact tracker.

\section{Risk anticipation}
\textbf{Performance-contract preservation.} The new torque-path branch inherits \emph{every} cap the scaffold enforced
because it \emph{reuses} \code{apply\_step} (the identical $\mathrm{clip}\to\times\slew\to$ governor $\to$ \code{step\_ablation}
stage); the offset enters \emph{before} that stage and is itself bounded ($c_k,\Delta\tau_T$ from a tanh-bounded actor
$\times$ fixed scales). Zero-$\theta$ is bit-exact $\pi_0$, so the worst case is never worse than the proven scaffold.
\textbf{The float-exactness trap.} A literal $\tau_{\mathrm{des}}$-tracker loses the $\theta{=}0$ identity to a
$/\slew\!\cdot\!\slew$ round-trip; the numerator-injection realisation avoids it and is the reason the identity gate is
bit-exact rather than ``$\approx$''. \textbf{The scientific trap.} Nominal K6 is necessary-not-sufficient ($\pi_0$ has it);
the claim rides entirely on paired panel improvement over the frozen scaffold in $\ge2/3$ seeds, on a panel fixed
\emph{before} any TD3 run and never tuned to results. \textbf{Preload random-walk (the retired failure).} Structurally
prevented: exploration is episode-fixed on $\theta$, and $\Delta\tau_T$ has an explicit fixed basis --- the
\code{TERMINAL\_OFFSET\_TRACKING} gate proves the executed preload matches the requested one under clipping/slew/contact.
\textbf{Worst-case input.} Production scale here $=$ the frozen dev panel (16 admissible READY / actuator-state
perturbations, \code{sig\_q}=0.02, \code{sig\_v}=0.06, \code{sig\_tau}=0.05) through the full downstream horizon 80 under
real mujoco 3.10, $\times$ 3 seeds $\times$ 400 options --- not a toy fixture; the 1-seed production smoke exercises exactly
this before the multi-seed queue.

\section{Gate ladder + stop logic}
\begin{enumerate}[nosep,leftmargin=1.6em]
  \item \textbf{(before training)} \code{TORQUE\_PATH\_ZERO\_DELTA\_IDENTITY\_PASS}, \code{TORQUE\_PATH\_BIT\_EXACT\_SCAFFOLD\_PASS},
  \code{OPTION\_ZERO\_POLICY\_K6\_PASS}. Any failure $\Rightarrow$ \halt{} (the coordinate is wrong; do not train).
  \item \textbf{(before training)} \code{TERMINAL\_OFFSET\_TRACKING\_PASS} $+$ \code{STRUCTURED\_THETA\_EXPLORATION\_ADMISSIBLE}:
  the smallest of $\le3$ pre-registered episodic scales giving \emph{both} preserved nominal successes and safe near-boundary
  failures and genuinely distinct trajectories, with executed $\Delta\tau_T$ matching the request
  (\code{NO\_PRELOAD\_RANDOM\_WALK}). If no scale is admissible $\Rightarrow$ \halt{} and re-scope the scales (not the
  coordinate).
  \item \textbf{(training)} \code{STRUCTURED\_OPTION\_TD3\_IMPROVES\_OVER\_SCAFFOLD\_PASS}: post-TD3 beats the frozen scaffold
  (paired, same panel members) in $\ge2/3$ seeds while \emph{preserving} nominal HOME\_V1 K6, safety, and every boundary
  contract. Else emit only \code{STRUCTURED\_OPTION\_TD3\_NO\_IMPROVEMENT\_WITHIN\_FROZEN\_BUDGET} --- \emph{never} an
  RL-impossibility claim.
\end{enumerate}
\textbf{Stop logic.} Each numbered gate is a \emph{boundary}: verify, report, and stop for review before the next. After
the TD3 verdict, commit and \halt{} --- no 24-seed reproduction (R10.3), no SAC/PPO (R10.4), no energy experiments. If a gate
fails, \halt{} and localise within the current coordinate; do not widen scope.
