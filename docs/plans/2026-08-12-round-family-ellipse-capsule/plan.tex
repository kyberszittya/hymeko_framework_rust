\section{Scope and goal}
Add the \textbf{round / smooth-non-circular} shape family --- the complement of the polygon corner family:
\textbf{O3-E}, a flat elliptical prism, and \textbf{O9-K}, a flat stadium (discorectangle) prism = the
planar analogue of a capsule. Both are smooth-rimmed (no corners), orientation-dependent, and equal-area
to the coin (mass $=$ O0). O3-E fills the roadmap's long-parked ``O3 ellipse'' slot.

\paragraph{Key recon result --- native geoms are wrong here.} MuJoCo's native \texttt{ellipsoid} and
\texttt{capsule} are 3-D rounded bodies that \emph{bulge in $z$}; matched in the plane they still have a
different \emph{volume} than the flat coin cylinder, so they do \textbf{not} give mass parity
(measured: ellipsoid $0.0335$, capsule $0.0344$ vs coin $0.0503$\,kg). They also break the flat-topped-puck
invariant every existing shape shares. $\Rightarrow$ both round shapes are built as \textbf{flat mesh
prisms} (a 2-D smooth boundary extruded to $\pm$half-thickness), exactly like the polygon prisms --- giving
equal-area mass parity and consistent flat-face contact.

\section{Geometry (verified numerically)}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item \textbf{Ellipse} O3-E: boundary $(\,r_x\cos t,\ r_y\sin t\,)$, $N{=}128$ segments, extruded. Semi-axes
    $r_x{=}$\texttt{radius}, $r_y{=}$\texttt{radius\_y}. Equal-area when $r_x r_y = r^2$. Chosen
    $r_x{=}0.025,\ r_y{=}0.016$ ($r_xr_y{=}4\times10^{-4}{=}r^2$, aspect $1.56{:}1$): mass $0.050246$
    ($\Delta_{O0}{=}2.0\times10^{-5}$), footprint $25.0$\,mm.
  \item \textbf{Capsule / stadium} O9-K: a rectangle with two semicircular caps of radius $b$; half-length
    $a$, $\rho{=}a/b$. Area $4(a{-}b)b + \pi b^2 = \pi r^2 \Rightarrow b = r\sqrt{\pi/(4(\rho{-}1)+\pi)}$.
    For $\rho{=}2$: $a{=}0.026533,\ b{=}0.013266$; $n_{\text{cap}}{=}48$ arc segments/cap: mass $0.050250$
    ($\Delta_{O0}{=}1.6\times10^{-5}$), footprint $26.5$\,mm. Flat sides + round ends (distinct from the
    all-curved ellipse).
\end{itemize}
Both $\Delta_{O0} < 10^{-4}$ (the mass-parity tolerance) --- clean shape-only ablations.

\section{Interface changes}
Additive: two new \texttt{Shape} members + two new geom branches. \textbf{No signature change} (ellipse/capsule
reuse \texttt{radius}/\texttt{radius\_y}). Existing shapes' code paths are untouched (new \texttt{elif}
branches only) --- so the regression risk is lower than the N-gon refactor.

\section{Test strategy}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item Unit (object\_spec): \texttt{from\_str} ellipse/capsule; preconditions (missing \texttt{radius\_y}
    asserts; capsule $a\le b$ asserts); \texttt{polygon\_sides()} is \texttt{None}; footprint values.
  \item Unit (object\_curriculum): membership (10); O3-E/O9-K geom \texttt{MESH}, mass $\approx$ O0, footprint.
  \item Integration: generation smoke over the full 10-variant curriculum --- 0 model/contract failures,
    \textbf{O0--O8 certified-capture counts unchanged} vs the N-gon run (the new branches must not perturb
    existing shapes), O3-E/O9-K generate + certify straddle.
  \item Static: \texttt{ruff}, \texttt{mypy --strict} on object\_spec; ownership-guard green.
  \item Performance: peak RSS $<$ 2\,GB; record wall.
\end{itemize}

\section{Performance budget \& risk}
Peak RSS $<$ 2\,GB (cap 16\,GB). Wall: 10-variant smoke $\approx 40$\,min CPU (60 rollouts). Risks:
(i) mass parity --- the mesh is an $N$-segment approximation; mitigated by $N{=}128$/$n_{\text{cap}}{=}48$
(measured $\Delta<2\times10^{-5}$) and asserted $<10^{-4}$. (ii) Existing-shape regression --- low (additive
branches); the smoke's O0--O8 count match is the gate; halt if any drifts. (iii) Capsule degeneracy
($a\le b$) rejected by precondition. (iv) Worst-case input: full 10-variant smoke, no training.

\section{Rollback}
Single commit; revert drops the two \texttt{Shape} members, the two generators + branches, the two scenes,
the two curriculum entries. No state mutation.

\section{Out of scope}
No teacher / retrieval / RL / delivery --- generation + capture characterization only. Higher-eccentricity
ellipses / other stadia are trivial follow-ups (just new scene literals).
