\begin{abstract}
\noindent Stage 1A solved \code{HOME\_V1}$\rightarrow$\code{READY} analytically. Stage 1B closes the last, contact-rich
transition into the frozen downstream. The decisive reframing: the cradle handoff $(q^\star,\dot q^\star,\tau^\star)$ is
\emph{not} a global capture target but a \textbf{phase-point of a moving orbit} --- $q$ is the position and $\dot q$ the
\emph{tangent} of the same trajectory, $\tau^\star$ the control-history memory. Hitting all three jointly under a
terminal-state objective is ill-conditioned (position \emph{xor} velocity, measured). Instead we arrive \emph{moving
along the orbit's tangent}: a quintic terminal segment drives the tips from \code{READY} to a backward-tangent precursor
$q_{\mathrm{pre}}(\Delta t)=q^\star-\Delta t\,\dot q^\star$ with terminal velocity $\approx\dot q^\star$; the $\tau$
preload ramps \emph{during} that moving segment (applying $\tau^\star$ afterward accelerates the arm out of the
deliverable velocity band); and a short residual under a low-dimensional structured CEM closes the margin. The frozen
APPROACH$\rightarrow$HANDOFF\_RESET$\rightarrow$R2$\rightarrow$coast then generates the coupling itself. Positive control
already in hand: \textbf{strict K6 reproduced 3/3 independent planner seeds} (min $d_{tz}$ 2.0/9.7/10.9\,mm, safe, distinct
parameters), no state edit, no snapshot injection. This plan formalises it and freezes the traces. \emph{Join the orbit;
do not reconstruct the terminal.}
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope and goal}
\textbf{Goal.} A deterministic structured planner that, from the frozen \code{READY} shell, drives a phase-shaped moving
precursor into the frozen downstream and delivers strict K6 --- reproducibly across $\ge 3$ planner seeds, teacher-free,
no state edit. \textbf{Chain:}
\begin{center}\itshape HOME\_V1 $\to$ frozen analytic transit $\to$ READY $\to$ phase-shaped moving precursor (quintic +
during-segment preload + short residual) $\to$ frozen APPROACH $\to$ HANDOFF\_RESET $\to$ R2 $\to$ strict K6.\end{center}
\textbf{Non-goals.} No TD3 (this is the positive control that gates it). No modification of the frozen downstream, the
analytic transit, or the physics/monitor. No re-characterisation of a different deliverable basin unless this fork
regresses (that is fork B, deferred).

\section{Risk anticipation}
\textbf{Performance-contract preservation.} The capture adds no new torque authority: every command passes the same
\code{step\_ablation}$\to$\code{govern\_torque} clip (slew, torque, joint/coin-speed caps). The residual is bounded
($\pm$slew) and enters inside that clip.
\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Worst-case / the load-bearing coupling.} $\tau^\star$ ($\lVert\cdot\rVert{=}2.67$) is the
\emph{accelerating} torque ($\dot q$ jumps $0.66\to1.07$ in one downstream step). Applying it \emph{after} the moving
segment drives velocity to $\sim 2\times$ the deliverable band (measured). Mitigation = ramp the preload \emph{during}
the decelerating quintic, with the terminal-velocity scale $s$ compensating; the CEM searches (preload\_start, $b_{\max}$,
$s$) jointly. The deliverable tube is tight on preload ($\Delta\tau\lesssim0.13$) and wide on direction (cos $0.85$--$1.0$)
--- the plan targets the wide axis loosely and the tight axis via the during-segment ramp. The precursor must keep the
coin canonical until legal contact (checked) and stay safe (\code{peak\_qdot}$\le3$, \code{peak\_coin\_speed}$\le1.5$).
\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Reproducibility.} $\ge3$ \emph{independent} seeds must each deliver, and preferably not the
same parameter vector (the 3 measured differ: $s\in\{0.08,0.28,0.31\}$). Seeds, chosen parameters, and full causal traces
are saved.
